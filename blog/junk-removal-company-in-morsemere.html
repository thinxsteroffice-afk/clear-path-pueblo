What Junk Removal Typically Costs Around Pueblo

Junk removal is usually priced by how much of the truck your items fill, not by the hour. A standard junk truck holds about 12 to 15 cubic yards, roughly the volume of six to eight pickup beds. The ranges below are typical across the Pueblo market. Your actual price comes from a quote after we see the load.

  • One bulky item (a recliner or a mattress): often $75 to $175
  • About one-quarter of a truck (a small room's worth): often $150 to $300
  • About half a truck (a one-car garage that's partly full): often $300 to $500
  • A full truck: often $550 to $850+

Things that push the price up:

  • Heavy materials. Concrete, dirt, roofing shingles, and rock hit landfill weight limits long before they fill the truck. A half-load of broken concrete can cost more than a full load of furniture.
  • Refrigerant appliances. Refrigerators, freezers, and window AC units need their refrigerant recovered under EPA Section 608 rules before disposal, which often adds $25 to $75 per unit.
  • Electronics. Colorado has banned TVs, computers, and monitors from landfills since July 1, 2013, so they go to e-waste recycling, which has its own fees.
  • Hard access. Basements, second floors, and long carries across a 1-acre lot add labor time.

> The cheapest junk removal quote is often the one where you fill the truck. Paying for a full load and using half of it is the most common way people overpay.

When ClearPath Is NOT Worth It (Read This Before You Call)

We're putting this section in because it will cost us some jobs, and we'd rather lose those than leave a customer feeling we oversold them.

1. You're not in our service area. If you searched from Morsemere or anywhere in New Jersey, we can't help you. Even within Colorado, places more than about 45 to 60 minutes from Pueblo West, such as north Colorado Springs, are usually better served by a local company. The drive time ends up in your price.

2. You have one small item and a pickup truck. If you can lift it with one other person and you own a truck, hauling it yourself is almost always cheaper. Landfill gate fees for a pickup load are usually a fraction of a $75 to $175 minimum charge. Paying us to move one end table doesn't make sense.

3. You have a big remodel spread over weeks. If you're producing debris for 2 to 4 weeks, a roll-off dumpster you fill at your own pace often beats several junk pickups. A 20-yard dumpster sitting in your driveway for a week can cost less than three separate truck visits. We'll tell you that if we see it.

4. Your stuff is still sellable. A working washer, a decent dining set, or tools in good shape might bring $50 to $400 on Facebook Marketplace or at a Pueblo West yard sale. If you have 2 or 3 weeks before move-out, sell first and call us for what's left.

5. It's hazardous waste. We don't take paint thinner, pool chemicals, pesticides, motor oil, propane tanks, or asbestos-containing material. Those need a household hazardous waste facility or a licensed abatement contractor. Anyone who says they'll "just toss it in" is taking a legal shortcut with your name on it.

6. You need it gone in the next 2 hours on a Saturday. We often do same-day or next-day work, but not always. In spring and early summer, when cleanup season peaks, lead times can stretch to 2 to 4 days. If your deadline can't move, ask about timing on the first call before you count on us.

7. You want someone to sort and organize, not just remove. We haul, and we can set aside items you point out for donation. We're not professional organizers. Sorting 30 years of family paperwork is slow, careful work that you or a dedicated organizer should do before the truck arrives.
