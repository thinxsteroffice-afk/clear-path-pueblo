JUNK REMOVAL: ST. LOUIS TO PUEBLO

If you're searching for junk removal companies in St. Louis, ClearPath Junk Removal can't help you there. We work out of Pueblo West, Colorado, which is about 860 miles and 13 hours by road from St. Louis, and our crews only work in southern Colorado. For a St. Louis job, hire a local St. Louis hauler. What we can help with is the case we hear about every month: people moving between St. Louis and Pueblo West. If you're moving here from Missouri, or you own property here while you live there, this page covers what's worth hauling 860 miles, what to get rid of before the truck leaves, and how we handle the Pueblo West side.

Why a St. Louis Search Ends Up on a Pueblo West Page

Search engines often mix up locations, and people search from one city for a job in another. Here are the three situations we see most:

  • You're relocating to Pueblo West and found us while comparing junk haulers at both ends of the move.
  • You inherited or own a Pueblo West property but live in the St. Louis area, and you need someone local to clear it while you're not here.
  • You're leaving Pueblo West for Missouri and need the house emptied before closing.

If none of these fits you, the rest of this page is not for you. Search for a hauler with a St. Louis address and phone number, and check that they list the neighborhoods or counties you need.

The Math on Hauling Stuff 860 Miles

This is the part most moving checklists skip. Long-distance movers price by weight and distance. For a move like St. Louis to Pueblo West, expect roughly $0.60 to $1.00 per pound of shipped goods, depending on the season and the carrier. A typical 3-bedroom household is around 7,000 to 9,000 pounds, so many families pay $4,500 to $8,000 for this route.

So everything you load has a per-pound shipping cost. Some examples:

  • A 150-pound sleeper sofa costs about $90 to $150 to ship. A used one often resells for less than that.
  • An old 250-pound chest freezer costs $150 to $250 to move, and a new one costs about $250 to $400.
  • A 300-pound treadmill that hasn't been used since 2021 costs $180 to $300 just to keep.
  • Boxes of paper files weigh about 30 to 40 pounds each. Ten boxes of old tax records can cost $250 or more to move.

If you're driving a rental truck yourself, the numbers change but the idea is the same. A one-way 26-foot truck on this route often costs $1,800 to $3,000. At 8 to 10 miles per gallon, the trip uses about 86 to 108 gallons of fuel, which is $300 to $400 more. Dropping from a 26-foot truck to a 20-foot truck can save several hundred dollars on its own.

> The cheapest item to move is the one you got rid of in St. Louis. The second cheapest is the one you buy after you arrive in Pueblo West.

What to Purge Before You Leave St. Louis

You'll need a St. Louis hauler, donation pickup, or curbside service for this part. Here's what we suggest getting rid of before the move:

  • Particleboard furniture. It often breaks in transit, and it costs less to replace than to ship.
  • Large older appliances. Many Pueblo West rentals and resale homes already come with a washer, dryer, and fridge. Ask your agent before you ship yours.
  • Humidifiers and damp-climate gear. Pueblo West gets around 12 inches of precipitation a year, compared with about 41 inches in St. Louis. You'll want a humidifier here, not a dehumidifier.
  • Snow equipment beyond the basics. Pueblo West gets snow, but it usually melts within 1 to 3 days under the high-altitude sun. A shovel is enough. A big two-stage snowblower usually isn't worth bringing.
  • Lawn equipment sized for a green Missouri lawn. Many Pueblo West lots are 0.5 acres or more and landscaped for dry conditions. A riding mower set up for thick fescue may not suit your new yard.

Typical Pueblo West Pricing (So You Can Budget From Missouri)

Junk removal around here is priced by how much of the truck your items fill, not by the hour. These are typical southern Colorado ranges for 2026. Your quote will depend on weight, how easy the items are to reach, and disposal fees:

  • Single bulky item (couch, mattress, recliner): about $95 to $175
  • Quarter truckload: about $175 to $275
  • Half truckload: about $300 to $425
  • Full truckload (roughly 15 cubic yards): about $500 to $700
  • Appliances containing refrigerant (fridges, freezers, AC units): often an extra $25 to $50 because the refrigerant has to be removed properly before disposal
  • Hot tub removal: commonly $300 to $600, depending on access and whether we cut it up on site

When Hiring Us Isn't Worth It

Here are the cases where you shouldn't hire us, or shouldn't hire anyone for junk removal:

  • You're in St. Louis. We won't drive 860 miles for a cleanout. No out-of-area hauler can match local pricing, because travel time alone would cost more than the job.
  • You have one or two items and a pickup truck. Pueblo County residents can take loads to the landfill themselves, and the gate fee for a small pickup load is usually much less than our minimum. If you have the time and a truck bed, do it yourself.
  • Your stuff can be resold. If your furniture is in good shape, sell it before calling us. A decent dining set can bring $200 to $500 on local marketplace listings. We donate usable items when we can, but we don't pay you for them, so you get more by selling.
  • You need hazardous waste removed. We do not haul paint in quantity, chemicals, fuel, asbestos, or medical waste. That's not about pricing; it's against the law and against landfill rules. Pueblo County has household hazardous waste collection options for this.
  • You want a same-day crew for a full house during the first or last week of the month. Move-out weeks fill our schedule. Plan on booking 5 to 10 days ahead around month-end. If someone promises a same-day full-house cleanout that week, ask how many crews they have.
  • The job is mostly dirt, concrete, or rock. A standard junk truck isn't the right tool for that. Heavy material usually needs equipment and a separate rate structure, covered on [concrete rock removal pueblo](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo) and [skid steer services pueblo](/services/skid-steer-services-pueblo). Ask first, or the quote will surprise you.

Remote jobs also have a practical limit. When the owner isn't here, we can't read your mind about sentimental items. If anything in the house might matter to you, such as photos, paperwork, or a specific piece of jewelry, tag it or list it before we start. After the truck leaves for the landfill, those items can't be recovered.

How to Vet Any Junk Removal Company, Wherever You Are

These checks apply whether you're hiring in St. Louis or in Pueblo West:

  • Ask for the price structure in writing. "By volume" or "by the hour" should be stated up front, along with surcharges for mattresses, tires, and appliances.
  • Ask where the load goes. A legitimate hauler can name its disposal sites. Illegal dumping can come back on the property owner.
  • Confirm insurance. Crews carrying a 200-pound dresser down stairs can damage walls and floors.
  • Check that they actually serve your area. A real local business lists the towns it covers. Ours are on [areas](/areas).
