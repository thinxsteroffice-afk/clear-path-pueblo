What Appliance Removal Actually Costs in Pueblo West

Pricing for one appliance depends on three things: weight, refrigerant and access. Here is what typical ranges look like in this area:

  • Refrigerator or chest freezer: about $95–$175. These run 200–350 lbs, and refrigerant recovery adds handling cost.
  • Washer or dryer: about $75–$125 each. Most weigh 100–230 lbs, with no refrigerant.
  • Electric or gas range: about $75–$125. A gas line has to be capped first (more on that below).
  • Water heater: about $85–$150. A 40–50 gallon tank weighs 100–150 lbs empty and far more if it hasn't been drained.
  • Window AC unit: about $50–$90. It's small, but it still holds refrigerant.

Several items at once are where the price per item drops. If you're clearing a washer, dryer and old garage fridge together, you're usually paying for a share of the truck, not three separate trips. Bundled loads often come in 25–40% below what the same items would cost booked one at a time. The Refrigerant Rule Most People Don't Know About

Under Section 608 of the federal Clean Air Act, refrigerant in fridges, freezers, dehumidifiers and AC units has to be recovered by a certified technician before the appliance is scrapped. Most scrap yards won't take a refrigerant appliance unless it comes with documentation that the refrigerant was removed, or they'll charge you to remove it.

This is the main thing missing from most "how to get rid of a fridge" advice. You can't just drive a refrigerator to a scrap yard and expect cash. Many yards in southern Colorado will either:

1. Refuse it outright, 2. Charge a refrigerant-recovery fee, commonly $15–$40, or 3. Accept it only with a signed certification tag showing the refrigerant is gone.

If you cut the lines yourself to "drain" it, you're releasing a regulated substance and also making the unit harder for anyone to process. Don't do that.

Washers, dryers, ranges and water heaters don't carry refrigerant, so any metal recycler will take them without this step.

Prep Checklist Before Pickup

A few minutes of prep saves time and avoids surprise charges:

  • Empty and defrost fridges and freezers 24 hours ahead. A freezer with an ice block in it can weigh 30–50 lbs more, and meltwater all over your floor isn't fun for anyone.
  • Disconnect water lines on fridges with ice makers and on washers. Shut the supply valve first. A ¼-inch fridge line can still leak for a while.
  • Have gas appliances capped by a qualified person. We don't disconnect gas ranges or gas dryers. If a gas line is still attached when we arrive, we can't move the appliance. A plumber or HVAC tech typically charges $75–$150 for a disconnect-and-cap visit.
  • Drain the water heater if you can. A full 50-gallon tank weighs over 400 lbs.
  • Clear a path. Take doors off hinges if a fridge barely fits through. Move vehicles out of the garage, and tell us about steps or tight turns when you book.

When Appliance Removal Isn't Worth Paying For

Here's the section that loses us business. Skip paying a junk removal company if any of these apply to you:

You're buying a new appliance from a big-box retailer. Most major retailers offer haul-away when they deliver a new appliance, usually free or for about $0–$40. If your new fridge is arriving next Tuesday anyway, let the delivery crew take the old one. Do check the fine print. Most will only take a unit that's already disconnected and empty, and some only take a like-for-like swap (a fridge for a fridge, not a fridge plus an old dryer).

Your utility is running a recycling rebate. Energy utilities in Colorado have periodically offered free pickup plus a cash rebate, historically around $50, for working secondary fridges and freezers. Programs come and go, and Pueblo West is served by Black Hills Energy rather than Xcel, so check what's active for your account before you pay anyone. When a program like that is running, it's strictly better than hiring us.

You have a truck, a second person and a non-refrigerant appliance. A dryer or washer is about $10–$25 of scrap steel at current light-iron prices. If you can load it yourself, a scrap yard may pay you a little instead of you paying us. That only works for washers, dryers, ranges and water heaters. For fridges, see the refrigerant section above.

It still works and it's under 8–10 years old. Post it on Facebook Marketplace or a Pueblo West community group as "free, you haul." A working fridge or washer listed free usually gets picked up within 1–3 days. Habitat for Humanity ReStores also accept some working appliances, depending on condition and age. We do donate usable items when we can, but a free listing costs you nothing.

Your HOA or landlord is responsible. Renters, and owners in some covenant-controlled pockets, should check whether the landlord or HOA handles appliance replacement and removal before paying out of pocket.
