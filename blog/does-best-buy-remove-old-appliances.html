DOES BEST BUY REMOVE OLD APPLIANCES

Yes — Best Buy will haul away your old appliance, but only under conditions that leave a lot of Pueblo West households out. Two paths exist. If you're buying a replacement appliance from Best Buy and paying for delivery and installation, they will take the matching old unit away, and standard haul-away runs roughly $30 per item on top of delivery, though promotions sometimes fold it in free. If you aren't buying anything, Best Buy sells a standalone haul-away service at about $199.99 for up to two large items, available only where their delivery agents route. The catch for Pueblo West: Best Buy has no store in Pueblo County. The nearest locations are in Colorado Springs, roughly 45 miles north, and standalone haul-away coverage this far south is inconsistent.

What Best Buy Actually Takes, and What It Costs

Best Buy runs three separate programs, and people conflate them constantly.

Haul-away with delivery. Buy a new refrigerator, washer, dryer, range, dishwasher, or freezer, choose delivery, and add haul-away for the old one. The published add-on is around $29.99 per appliance. During Black Friday and Memorial Day appliance events, Best Buy has bundled free haul-away on units over $399 — those promos are real but they run maybe 6 to 8 weeks a year total.

Standalone haul-away. No purchase required. Best Buy charges $199.99 to pick up and remove up to two large items from your home. That's the number most people are shocked by. It's priced to be a convenience service for existing customers, not a competitive junk removal rate.

In-store recycling. Free, but only for small stuff — cables, batteries, small electronics, and in most states a limited number of TVs and monitors per household per day. You are not carrying a 280-pound side-by-side into a store.

> The $199.99 standalone fee is Best Buy telling you politely that appliance haul-away isn't their business. It's their delivery trucks doing a favor at a price that makes it worth their while.

The Pueblo West Problem Nobody Mentions

Best Buy's haul-away is tied to delivery routing, not to store proximity in the way people assume. The nearest Best Buy stores are on North Academy Boulevard and at the Chapel Hills area in Colorado Springs. Delivery to Pueblo and Pueblo West happens, but it happens on a schedule — often one or two routing days per week rather than daily.

Practical consequences for a household on Purcell, McCulloch, or out past Joe Martinez:

  • Delivery windows are 4 hours wide, and the confirmation call comes the evening before, not a week out. Take a half day off work.
  • Reschedules are common when the truck runs long in the Springs. Your Pueblo West stop is at the tail of the route, not the front.
  • Standalone haul-away ZIP coverage for 81007 has been spotty. Enter the ZIP at checkout and it may simply not offer the service.
  • Two-item cap. If you're clearing a garage with an old fridge, a dead chest freezer, and a washer, that's two orders and $399.98.

The Rules That Kill the Free Haul-Away

This is where most cancellations happen on delivery day. Best Buy's third-party delivery crews follow a strict list, and they will leave with your new appliance still on the truck if any of it isn't satisfied.

  • One-for-one, like-for-like. They take away a washer when they deliver a washer. They will not take your old dryer because you bought a fridge.
  • Disconnection is your job. Water lines, gas lines, and dryer vents must be disconnected before arrival. Delivery crews are not licensed to shut off gas in Colorado, and they will not touch a gas range that's still connected.
  • The fridge must be empty and defrosted, ideally 24 hours ahead. A freezer with two inches of ice in the bottom gets refused.
  • Clear path required. Snow on the walk, a step down into a sunken laundry room, a narrow hallway — any of these can void the haul-away.
  • No basements in many contracts. If the old washer is downstairs, a lot of delivery crews won't carry it up.
  • Refrigerant units only if intact. A punctured line, a missing compressor, or a unit already stripped for scrap gets declined as hazardous.

Add the sales tax picture too. Pueblo West sits in an unincorporated area of Pueblo County, and combined state and county sales tax lands near 5.4 percent, well below the roughly 8.2 percent inside Pueblo city limits. That's a real reason to buy locally and have it delivered here — but it doesn't fix the haul-away gap.

What This Actually Costs Compared to Alternatives

Here's the honest comparison for a single old refrigerator in Pueblo West:

| Option | Real cost | Real timeline | |---|---|---| | Best Buy haul-away with purchase | ~$30 | Tied to delivery date, often 5 to 14 days out | | Best Buy standalone haul-away | $199.99 for up to 2 items | If your ZIP is even covered | | Haul it yourself to the county landfill | ~$25 to $40 tipping plus truck rental at $19.95/day plus roughly $1.29/mile | One long Saturday, and you need two able bodies | | Scrap yard in Pueblo | You might get $8 to $20 for a steel appliance | Only if you can load it and refrigerant is legally handled | | Local junk removal | Typically $95 to $175 for one appliance | Often same-day or next-day |

The truck rental math surprises people. A 15-mile round trip from Pueblo West to the transfer station in a rented pickup is $19.95 plus about $19 in mileage plus fuel plus the tipping fee — you're at $60 to $80 before you've counted the hour and a half and the risk of a torn rotator cuff wrestling 280 pounds up a ramp.

Refrigerant is the part almost nobody accounts for. Federal Clean Air Act rules require that refrigerant be recovered by a certified technician before a fridge, freezer, or window AC unit is scrapped or landfilled. Dumping a sealed unit is not legal, and Pueblo County facilities charge a separate fee — commonly $15 to $30 — for Freon recovery on top of tipping. If a guy with a trailer offers to take your fridge for $40 cash, ask him what he does with the refrigerant. Most of the time the answer is nothing, and that's an illegal dump waiting to be traced back to a serial number.

The Real Failure Modes to Plan Around

  • The delivery-day cancellation. You took the day off, the crew arrives, the gas line is still connected, and now you have a new range in a box and an old one in the kitchen. This is the single most common Best Buy haul-away failure.
  • The two-item cap collision. Estate and downsizing situations routinely produce three to six appliances. Best Buy's structure isn't built for that. An [estate cleanout](/services/estate-cleanout-pueblo) handles it in one trip.
  • The ZIP code decline. You plan the whole thing around standalone haul-away, then discover at checkout that 81007 isn't served.
  • The "free with purchase" that wasn't. Promotional free haul-away often applies only to the primary appliance on multi-unit orders. Read which line item carries the credit.
  • Charity rejection. Habitat ReStore and similar donation programs in the Pueblo area accept appliances, but they will decline anything over 10 to 15 years old, anything non-working, and usually anything with a cosmetic dent. Plan for a no.
