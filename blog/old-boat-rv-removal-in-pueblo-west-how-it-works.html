OLD BOAT & RV REMOVAL IN PUEBLO WEST: HOW IT WORKS

Old boats, campers, and RVs have a way of becoming permanent yard fixtures. The registration lapsed years back, the engine won't turn over, the tires went flat, and now it's eating half your driveway or sitting dead behind the shed. Here's how we clear it out.

What we take

Fishing boats, jet skis, pop-up campers, truck campers, travel trailers, dead RVs, and the rusted-out trailers they sit on. If it hasn't moved in years, it's exactly the kind of job we do.

Why these jobs need a skid steer

A waterlogged hull or an RV with flat tires doesn't roll onto a truck. That's where most haulers turn a boat or camper down. We bring the skid steer with forks and bucket, so we can lift, drag, and load heavy dead weight without tearing up your yard or your driveway. Skid steer work in Pueblo is what lets us take the jobs other outfits won't.

How the removal works

1. Text a couple photos to (719) 334-4581 so we can see the size and how it's parked. 2. We give you a straight quote — not a "starting at" number that balloons later. 3. We schedule it, often same-day, and show up with two dump trailers and the skid steer. 4. We load it, sweep up what's underneath, and you get your space back.

What it costs

It depends on the size, the weight, and how boxed-in it is. A jet ski is a quick job. A full-size RV takes more work. We look before we quote, and the price we say is the price you pay. No surprise fees when the trailer's already loaded.

Where it goes

We handle the disposal right. Scrap metal gets recycled, and with 12-plus years of hazardous-material experience, we drain fuel, oil, and fluids properly instead of letting them leak in a landfill or your driveway.
