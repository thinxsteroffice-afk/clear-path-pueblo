GARAGE CLEAN OUT COST NEAR ME

A typical garage clean out in Pueblo West runs $250 to $750 for most two- and three-car garages, with the majority of our jobs landing between $325 and $575. Small jobs — a corner of clutter, six or eight items — start around $149. A packed three-car garage with a decade of accumulation, old shelving, and a dead appliance or two runs $750 to $1,200. Price is driven by *volume*, not hours: how much space your stuff takes up in the trailer, plus surcharges for heavy or restricted material. Most jobs are done in 90 minutes to 4 hours, same-day or next-day. Below is the actual breakdown, what pushes a quote up, and an honest section on when you should skip hiring anyone at all.

What You're Actually Paying For (Volume Pricing, Explained)

Nearly every junk removal company in Pueblo County prices by trailer or truck space. Ours is a dump trailer, and we quote in fractions:

  • 1/8 load (~2 cubic yards) — $149–$199. A workbench, some boxes, a bike, a few bags.
  • 1/4 load (~4 cu yd) — $225–$300. One bay's worth of clutter, or a small shed's contents.
  • 1/2 load (~8 cu yd) — $350–$475. The most common Pueblo West garage job.
  • 3/4 load (~12 cu yd) — $475–$625.
  • Full load (~16 cu yd) — $625–$850. A genuinely full two-car garage, floor to rafters.

That price includes labor, loading, sweeping, driving, and the tipping fee at the landfill. You don't lift anything and you don't pay separately for the dump run.

Two things people misjudge constantly. First, loose clutter takes up 2–3x the volume you think — an unbroken-down cardboard pile that looks like "half a pickup" is often a third of a trailer. Second, weight matters past a point. Volume pricing assumes household density (roughly 150–250 lbs per cubic yard). Concrete, brick, dirt, tile, roofing, and paver stacks run 2,500–4,000 lbs per cubic yard and get quoted separately — see [concrete and rock removal](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo). A half-trailer of broken sidewalk isn't $400, it's a different job on a different trailer.

What Pushes a Pueblo West Quote Up or Down

Local factors that actually move the number here, not generic ones:

  • Lot size and drive distance. Pueblo West lots run half an acre to 5+ acres, and a detached garage 200 feet off the driveway means every item is carried, not wheeled. That can add 20–40% in labor time. If we can back the trailer to the door, you're on the low end of the range.
  • Where you are in the district. Jobs off Purcell, McCulloch, Joe Martinez, and Industrial are quick for us. Out past the golf course toward the reservoir or up the north-end acreage roads adds 15–25 minutes each way, which we absorb on larger jobs and reflect on tiny ones.
  • Restricted items. Paint, solvents, pesticides, motor oil, propane tanks, and car batteries can't go in a mixed load. Pueblo County runs household hazardous waste drop-off events and a facility with limited hours; expect $0 for you to drop off yourself versus $15–$45 per item if we handle disposal routing. Half-full paint cans are the single most common thing that surprises people.
  • Appliances and freon. A fridge, freezer, or AC unit requires certified refrigerant recovery — typically $45–$95 each on top of load pricing. Details on [appliance removal](/services/appliance-removal-pueblo).
  • Tires. Colorado charges a disposal fee per tire; budget $8–$15 each, more for truck or tractor tires. Nearly every acreage garage out here has four to eight orphan tires in a corner.

> The number that surprises Pueblo West homeowners isn't the price of the cleanout — it's how much of what's in the garage is worthless to a buyer and expensive to throw away. Sun-baked plastic, warped particleboard, and 15-year-old paint all cost money to move and return nothing.

The Pueblo West Garage Problem Nobody Names

Two conditions here wreck stored goods faster than in Denver or the Front Range suburbs, and they change the math on "I'll sell it instead."

Unconditioned garages in Pueblo West routinely hit 125–140°F on a July afternoon under a black roof at 4,700 feet elevation, with UV load noticeably higher than at sea level. Latex paint stored through three summers is usually dead. Plastic totes go brittle and crack when lifted. Foam in stored patio cushions powders. Adhesives on cheap furniture let go.

Then there's the wind. Sustained 30–45 mph spring gusts push fine dust through every garage door gap in the district. Anything stored on open shelving for five years has a dust film that kills resale on electronics, tools, and fabric.

The practical result: on a typical garage we clear, roughly 10–20% of the contents have real resale value, another 10–15% are donatable, and the rest is disposal. Homeowners planning to "sell most of it first" usually spend six weekends recovering $150.

When a Garage Clean Out Service Is a Bad Buy

This is the part most companies leave off their pricing page. Do not hire us — or anyone — in these situations.

You have a pickup, a free Saturday, and less than a quarter-trailer. A single truckload to the landfill costs you roughly $25–$40 in minimum tipping fees plus fuel. Call ahead to confirm current rates and hours, since they change. If you're physically able and the load is light, you're paying us $225+ to save yourself about three hours. That's a defensible trade if your time is worth $75/hr or your back is bad. It's a bad trade if it isn't.

You haven't decided what to keep. Our crew cannot make keep/toss decisions for you. If you point at a garage and say "figure it out," either we stop every 90 seconds to ask, or something you wanted goes in the trailer. Jobs where the homeowner hasn't pre-sorted run 30–50% longer and generate almost all of our regret calls. Sort first — even a rough "this wall stays" line on the floor works. If sorting is the actual obstacle, hire an organizer, not a hauler.

You need multiple days and repeated access. A 10–20 yard roll-off dumpster rented for a week runs about $350–$550 in this area and lets you load at your own pace over a long weekend. For a slow whole-house purge, that beats hiring labor twice. We're better when you want it gone in one visit and you don't want a container sitting on your driveway.

Your items are genuinely valuable. Working tools, cast iron, a running snowblower, real wood furniture, and older Craftsman hand tools sell locally. If you have $800+ of sellable goods and the patience for a month of Marketplace messages, sell first, then call us for the remainder. Note the flip side: a $400 half-load quote does not drop to $200 because you removed the valuable 10% — volume barely changed.

The load is one heavy item. A single hot tub, one shed, or a pool deck isn't a garage cleanout and shouldn't be priced like one. See [hot tub removal](/services/hot-tub-removal-pueblo) or [shed demolition and removal](/services/shed-demolition-removal-pueblo) instead — quoting those as trailer fractions overcharges you.

You want your stuff "donated, not dumped," guaranteed. We take usable goods to local donation centers when they'll accept them, but thrift stores here reject stained upholstery, non-working electronics, cribs, most mattresses, and anything with visible mouse evidence. Realistically 50–70% of a garage load goes to the landfill no matter who you hire. Any company promising near-total diversion on a dusty Pueblo West garage is selling you a story.

Failure Modes and Real Limitations

  • We can't haul hazardous waste. Old fuel, pool chemicals, ammunition, medical sharps, and asbestos-containing material are hard nos. If your 1970s garage has vinyl-asbestos tile or wrapped ductwork, that's an abatement contractor, not us.
  • Same-day isn't guaranteed in season. March through June and September through October we book 2–5 days out. If you need it cleared before a Friday closing, call on Monday.
  • Estimates over the phone drift. A photo-based quote is accurate within about ±20%. We confirm the number on site before touching anything, and you can walk away at that point at no charge — but expect the on-site number to differ from the phone number when the garage has an attic or loft you didn't mention.
  • Rodent and biohazard conditions change the job. Heavy droppings, nesting, or contamination require PPE and different handling — that shifts into [hoarding cleanout](/services/hoarding-cleanout-pueblo) territory and costs more than standard rates.
  • We won't move things back into your garage. We remove. Reorganizing what stays is not part of the price.

How to Get an Accurate Number Fast

Do these four things and your quote will be within about 10% of final:

  • Take 4 photos: each wall plus one from the door with the light on.
  • Count the big stuff — appliances, mattresses, tires, propane tanks, paint cans.
  • Say whether the trailer can get within 30 feet of the door.
  • Flag anything concrete, dirt, or masonry separately.
