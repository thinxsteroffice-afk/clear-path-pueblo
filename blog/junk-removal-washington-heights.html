What junk removal costs in the Pueblo area

Most companies price by volume, meaning how much of the truck your load fills, not by the hour. A standard junk truck holds about 15 cubic yards. That's roughly 6 full-size pickup beds. These are typical Pueblo-area ranges:

| Load size | Rough equivalent | Typical range | |---|---|---| | Single item / minimum | 1 couch or 1 appliance | $95 – $175 | | 1/4 truck | Small bedroom set | $175 – $275 | | 1/2 truck | One-car garage, partly full | $300 – $425 | | 3/4 truck | Large basement cleanout | $425 – $575 | | Full truck | Whole-garage cleanout | $550 – $750 |

Some things raise the price:

  • Heavy materials. Concrete, dirt, rock and roofing shingles weigh 1 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard. Companies often charge by weight or by a smaller "heavy load" for these. See [concrete rock removal pueblo](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo).
  • Appliances with refrigerant. Refrigerators, freezers and window AC units have to go to a facility that recovers refrigerant under EPA Section 608 rules. Expect a $25 to $75 surcharge per unit from most haulers.
  • Hot tubs. A typical 6-person spa weighs 500 to 900 pounds empty and usually needs to be cut up on site. Most quotes come in at $300 to $600. Details are at [hot tub removal pueblo](/services/hot-tub-removal-pueblo).
  • Stairs and long carries. A second-floor bedroom set takes longer than a curbside pile. Some companies add $25 to $50 for that.

> The price you agree to before loading should be the price you pay. If a company won't commit to a number until the truck is full, assume it will go up.

How to tell a good junk removal company from a bad one

You don't have to take our word for any of this. Here are six checks you can run on any hauler in Pueblo County, including us:

1. Written, all-in price before loading. It should include disposal fees, fuel and labor. "Plus dump fees" with no number attached is a red flag. 2. Proof of liability insurance. Crews carry 300-pound appliances through doorways and over hardwood floors. Ask for the certificate. Real companies send it the same day. 3. A plan for donations and recycling. Ask where the furniture goes. A company that hauls everything straight to the landfill is often charging you disposal fees on items that could have been donated for free. 4. Electronics handled legally. Colorado has banned TVs, computers, monitors and printers from landfills since July 1, 2013. Your hauler should take them to an e-waste recycler, and many charge $10 to $40 per screen to cover it. 5. Arrival windows, not "sometime Tuesday." A 2-hour window is reasonable. An 8-hour window wastes your day. 6. Reviews that mention problems. A perfect score with no detail tells you less than a 4.8 where the owner answers complaints in public.

When hiring a junk removal company ISN'T worth it

This section may cost us jobs. Here are the cases where you shouldn't pay us, or anyone else, to haul your junk.

You're not in our area. If you meant Washington Heights in New York City, or anywhere outside the Pueblo region, we can't serve you. A company that says it can take jobs everywhere is usually a lead broker that resells your request to a stranger. Hire someone local to you.

It's one item and you own a pickup. One recliner or one mattress, a truck, and a free Saturday morning add up to about $15 to $40 in landfill fees, compared with a $95 to $175 minimum from us. For a single light item, haul it yourself.

Your project runs for weeks. For a kitchen remodel or a roof tear-off that produces debris every day for 2 to 4 weeks, a roll-off dumpster usually costs less. A 10- to 20-yard container typically runs $350 to $600 for a week, and you fill it at your own pace. We'd charge for each trip. Once the project is done, though, we're often cheaper for the final cleanup. See [construction debris removal pueblo](/services/construction-debris-removal-pueblo).

Your stuff still has value. If you're clearing out good furniture, working appliances or tools, sell them first. Facebook Marketplace and local buy-sell groups move decent items in Pueblo within days. Scrap metal like old water heaters, copper and aluminum can pay you a few dollars at a scrap yard instead of costing you a haul fee. Call us for what's left after that.

It's hazardous waste. We don't take, and legally can't take:

  • Paint that's still liquid, solvents, pesticides and pool chemicals
  • Motor oil, gasoline and propane tanks with gas still in them
  • Asbestos materials, including old popcorn ceilings, some pre-1980 flooring and pipe wrap
  • Medical waste and anything biohazardous

Pueblo County residents can usually drop household hazardous waste at county collection events. A company that offers to "just toss it in" with the rest of your load is breaking the law. You could share the liability if it shows up at the landfill.

You need it gone in the next 2 hours. We often do same-day pickups, but we can't promise them, especially Friday through Monday in spring and fall. If a closing or a move-out deadline is hours away, a rented trailer may be the safer bet.
