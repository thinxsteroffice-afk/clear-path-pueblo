GARAGE CLEANING NEAR ME: 24-72 HRS

Yes — garage cleanouts in Pueblo West are usually available within 24 to 72 hours, and roughly 3 out of 10 calls we take before 10 a.m. get a same-day afternoon slot. A standard two-car garage runs $400 to $650 and takes 2 to 4 hours with a two-person crew. Weekday mornings (7:30–11:30 a.m.) have the most open capacity; Saturdays book out 7 to 10 days in spring. Call or text with your cross streets — McCulloch, Purcell, Joe Martinez, Industrial — and a rough description ("two-car garage, half full, one old freezer") and we can give you a firm arrival window and a price range in about five minutes. Booking details are on our [garage cleanout page](/services/garage-cleanout-pueblo).

What "available" actually means on a real schedule

Most junk removal sites answer the availability question with "call now!" and no numbers. Here is the actual shape of a week in Pueblo West.

  • Monday and Tuesday mornings are the softest. We hold two crews and typically fill 60–70% of those slots, so same-day is realistic.
  • Wednesday and Thursday fill with commercial and rental-turnover work. Same-day drops to maybe one job in five.
  • Fridays are the tightest weekday — movers close on Fridays, and cleanouts pile onto them.
  • Saturdays are booked 7 to 10 days out from March through June, and 3 to 5 days out in January and February.
  • Last three days of the month are the single worst window all year for availability. Lease-ends and closings collide. If your date is flexible, avoid the 28th through the 31st.

We run a 2-hour arrival window, not a "sometime today." The reason is the drive: a loaded trailer from most Pueblo West addresses to the disposal site and back is 35 to 45 minutes of round trip, and a garage that fills the truck means a mid-job dump run that no dispatcher can predict to the minute. If someone promises you an exact time to the minute for a garage cleanout, they are guessing.

What a two-car garage actually costs here

Pricing is by volume, not by hour, and our truck holds about 15 cubic yards — roughly six pickup-bed loads.

  • Minimum charge: $175. That covers a single-item grab or a small corner pile. Below that we lose money on fuel and dump fees.
  • Quarter load: $250–$350. Think one wall of a garage, some boxes, a bike, a busted lawn chair set.
  • Half load: $400–$550. The most common Pueblo West garage job.
  • Full load: $650–$900. A packed two-car with shelving, an old workbench, and years of "I'll deal with it later."
  • Overweight surcharge: we add $45 to $85 when a load runs heavy on concrete chunks, roofing tear-off, or dirt, because the transfer station charges us by the ton, not by the yard.

Add-ons worth knowing: an old chest freezer or fridge is $45–$75 extra because refrigerant units get handled separately. A hot tub sitting behind the garage is a whole different job — see [hot tub removal](/services/hot-tub-removal-pueblo). Pallets of leftover flatwork or a broken-up slab go under [concrete and rock removal](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo).

> We would rather lose a $175 job than take money for something you could handle with a trailer and one Saturday. If we tell you to skip us, that is us being accurate, not modest.

When you should NOT hire us

This is the section most companies leave out. Read it before you call.

You have a truck, a free Saturday, and under a pickup bed of junk. The Pueblo County disposal site charges a modest per-load minimum for household waste — call ahead for the current rate, since it changes. Even at the high end, you are out maybe $30 to $50 plus fuel. Our minimum is $175. Hiring us for that is paying about $130 for two hours of your own time back. That is a fine trade for some people and a bad one for others. You know which you are.

You are cleaning out and also renovating. If you are pulling drywall, framing, and flooring over three weekends, a 20-yard roll-off dumpster at roughly $375–$500 for a 7-day rental beats calling us four separate times at $400 a pop. Dumpsters win on multi-day, self-paced projects. We win on one-day, hands-off, "I don't want to touch it" projects. Also check whether your street or the Pueblo West Metro District covenants restrict a container sitting in a driveway for a week — that catches people.

You want us to sort, organize, and set up shelving. We are a removal crew, not professional organizers. We haul what you point at. If your real goal is a functional garage with labeled bins and a workbench that works, hire an organizer for $60–$90 an hour and use us only for the discard pile at the end. Calling us to "help decide" turns a 3-hour job into a 6-hour one and doubles your bill.

You are hoping we'll resell your stuff and cut you a check. We don't. Anything genuinely valuable — a running snowblower, a decent table saw, quality tools — is worth more to you on Facebook Marketplace than in our trailer. Sell the top 5 items first. On a typical Pueblo West garage, that is often $200 to $600 you keep, and it lightens the load enough to drop you from a full to a half load.

The garage is a symptom, not the problem. If the garage is packed because the house is packed, a garage-only cleanout gets refilled within a year. That is a different conversation, handled honestly on our [hoarding cleanout page](/services/hoarding-cleanout-pueblo). We will still do the garage. We just won't pretend it fixed anything.

You need it done in the next 3 hours on a Friday in May. Sometimes availability just isn't there. We will tell you no rather than show up at 6 p.m. with one guy and a half-empty trailer.

Failure modes we've actually hit

  • The scope doubles on arrival. Photos over text show one wall. We arrive and the attic above the garage is full too. Expect a re-quote on site — we won't start and then surprise you at the end, but the price will change.
  • Weather kills the window. Pueblo West wind is not a rumor. Sustained gusts make an open trailer of loose insulation, cardboard, or foam genuinely unsafe on US-50. We reschedule, usually to the next morning.
  • Nobody's home and nothing's decided. "Take whatever's in the left bay" works. "I'll be at work, figure it out" does not. Undecided items get left, and you call us again.
  • Hidden weight. A garage that looks like boxes but is actually 40 boxes of books, tile, or old paint cans will price like a full load at half the visible volume.
  • Access. Detached garages on the bigger Pueblo West lots sometimes sit 60–100 feet back with a soft dirt approach. If we can't get a trailer within about 40 feet, add 30 to 60 minutes of carry time.

Things we can't take — and where they go

Not everything is haulable, and knowing this ahead of time saves you a wasted appointment:

  • Paint, solvents, pesticides, motor oil — household hazardous waste. Pueblo County runs periodic collection events; check current dates before you plan around one.
  • Tires — most disposal sites charge per tire and many haulers, us included, either decline them or add a per-tire fee.
  • Propane tanks, ammunition, and pressurized cylinders — hard no, every time.
  • Anything suspected to contain asbestos — old floor tile, certain pipe wrap, some siding. That requires a licensed abatement contractor, not a junk crew. Guessing wrong here is a five-figure mistake.
  • Full appliances still under a warranty claim — sounds odd, but people do call us and then find out the retailer would have hauled it free. Check first. Otherwise, see [appliance removal](/services/appliance-removal-pueblo).

How to get the earliest slot

Availability is partly luck and partly how you ask. What moves you up the list:

1. Text photos, not descriptions. Three wide shots of the garage with the door open beats a paragraph. We quote faster and more accurately, which means we can slot you into a gap the same day. 2. Give a window, not a time. "Any morning this week" gets served days before "Thursday at 1 p.m." 3. Pre-stage the obvious. Anything you already know is going — drag it to the apron. A pre-staged half load can drop from 3 hours to 90 minutes, which sometimes turns a "no" into a "we can squeeze you in at 2." 4. Book off-peak. The first two weeks of a month, October through February, are our lightest stretch of the year. Same-day is common then. 5. Ask about combining. If a neighbor on your street also needs a haul, we can do both on one trip and often knock $50–$100 off each.

What the day looks like

Crew arrives inside the window, walks the garage with you, confirms the price before touching anything, and loads. Two people, 2 to 4 hours for a typical two-car. We sweep the floor. Metal goes to a scrap yard rather than the landfill — on a garage job that is usually 10–20% of the load by weight, and it is why we can hold pricing where we do. Payment on completion.

If you also have yard piles, a dead shed, or an old play set out back, mention it at booking — it changes the trailer we bring.
