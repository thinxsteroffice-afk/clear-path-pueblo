LAWN MOWER & SNOWBLOWER REMOVAL IN PUEBLO WEST

November is when a lot of Pueblo West garages hit their limit. The mower is done for the year, the snowblower has to come out front, and there's the old one that quit back in 2023 still sitting in the corner. If that sounds like your place, here's how getting rid of small-engine equipment actually works.

What We Haul

If it has a small engine and doesn't run anymore, or you just don't want it, we'll take it:

  • Push mowers and self-propelled mowers
  • Riding mowers and lawn tractors
  • Snowblowers, tillers and edgers
  • Weed eaters, leaf blowers and chainsaws
  • Old gas cans, oil jugs and the parts bin next to them

Most of this is metal, so it goes to scrap recycling instead of the landfill.

The Gas and Oil Question

This is the part people worry about, and it's a fair question. Old gas and motor oil shouldn't go down a drain, onto the gravel, or into your trash can. Please don't try to tip a riding mower over in the driveway to empty it.

Just tell us what's in it when you call. Our family has handled hazardous material for more than 12 years. We know how to deal with fuel and oil safely and get it where it needs to go. If you've got half-empty gas cans or old oil sitting around, mention those too.

Riding Mowers and the Heavy Stuff

A lawn tractor can weigh 400 to 600 pounds, and the dead ones don't roll easily. Sometimes it's got a flat tire, sometimes it's been sitting behind the shed in the weeds for years. That's when we bring the skid steer. Pueblo jobs like that get done in one visit with the forks instead of three guys and a busted ramp. It's the same equipment we use for hot tub removal in Pueblo, so a mower doesn't give it any trouble.

What It Costs

We price by how much room your stuff takes up in the trailer and how much work it takes to load it. One push mower is a small job. Most people save money by doing it all at once: the mower, the broken snowblower, that pile of yard debris Pueblo winds left along the fence, and anything else that's been crowding the garage. If it turns into a full garage cleanout, Pueblo customers get one quote up front and no surprise fees.
