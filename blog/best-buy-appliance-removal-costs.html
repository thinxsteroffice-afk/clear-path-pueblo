BEST BUY APPLIANCE REMOVAL: COSTS

Best Buy will haul away your old appliance for $49.99 if they're delivering a replacement you bought from them. If you aren't buying anything, their standalone Haul Away and Recycle service costs $199.99 and takes up to 2 large items (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers, freezers, microwaves, TVs) plus unlimited small electronics. For most Pueblo West homeowners buying a new appliance, the $49.99 add-on is the cheapest option and you should take it. The $199.99 service is a different story. It has limits that catch people off guard: crews won't disconnect anything, it won't take furniture or fitness equipment, businesses can't use it, and it only covers two big items. Below is when Best Buy is the right call, when it isn't, and what else Pueblo West has.

What Best Buy's Appliance Haul-Away Actually Covers

The closest Best Buy is at 5890 N Elizabeth St in Pueblo, and delivery crews serve Pueblo West from there. Here's how the two options compare, based on Best Buy's published terms:

  • With a purchase ($49.99 per item): They haul away one old appliance for each replacement they deliver. It covers cooktops, dishwashers, dryers, freezers, ice makers, microwaves, ranges, range hoods, refrigerators, wall ovens, and washers.
  • Standalone ($199.99): Up to 2 large products total, plus unlimited small electronics like laptops, printers, game consoles, and speakers. No purchase needed.
  • Relocation ($49.99 per appliance): If you'd rather have the old fridge moved to the garage than hauled off, that's also $49.99.
  • In-store drop-off: Best Buy's own FAQ says it "does not accept large appliances in stores." You can't load a washer into your truck and drop it at the Elizabeth St store.

The in-store recycling limit is 10 qualifying items per household per day, with no more than 5 laptops, 2 TVs or monitors, and 3 other items. That's useful for electronics and does nothing for a refrigerator.

The Fine Print That Trips Up Pueblo West Homeowners

This is where the Best Buy option gets harder than the price suggests. Best Buy's haul-away terms say crews will not:

  • Disconnect or uninstall any product
  • Remove doors, door frames, molding, trim, or railings to get an appliance out
  • Modify cabinets, flooring, or walls
  • Take any item that's a health or safety hazard, or one with food, clothing, dishes, or pests still inside

If you didn't pay for installation, the old appliance has to be fully disconnected before the truck shows up. For a refrigerator, that means unplugged, emptied, and ideally defrosted for 24 hours so it doesn't leak across your floor. For a washer, the water supply is shut off, the hoses are off, and the drum is drained. For a gas range or dryer, a licensed plumber or your gas utility needs to shut off and cap the line. Don't do that yourself, and don't expect a delivery crew to do it.

A lot of Pueblo West homes are ranch-style with the laundry in the garage or a mudroom, which usually makes this manageable. But if your 1990s side-by-side fridge got installed before the kitchen remodel and won't fit through the new doorway, Best Buy won't pull the trim off to get it out.

> Once Best Buy hauls an item away, it's gone. Their terms say it plainly: removed products "can't be returned." If there's any chance you'll want that chest freezer back for hunting season, don't put it on the truck.

When Best Buy (or Anyone Else) Is the Better Choice Than Us

We're a junk removal company, and we'd like your business. But in several common situations, hiring us is the wrong call, and you should know that before you pick up the phone.

You're buying a new appliance from Best Buy anyway. Pay the $49.99. No local hauler is going to send a truck and two people to Pueblo West for one appliance and charge less than that. If your whole project is "new fridge in, old fridge out," the add-on is the right answer.

Your old refrigerator or freezer still works. Black Hills Energy runs an appliance recycling program for Colorado electric customers. It picks up working refrigerators and freezers between 10 and 30 cubic feet and pays you a rebate, with an extra $50 if you add a working window air conditioner. The catches: pickups usually run about once a month, households are limited to 2 pickups per calendar year, and units that use ammonia or SO2 refrigerant don't qualify. If you can wait a few weeks, getting paid beats paying anyone. Check the current rebate amount on Black Hills' site before you schedule, because program budgets change.

It's a stove, washer, or dryer, and you have a truck. Appliance King at 305 W. Northern Ave in Pueblo recycles stoves, washers, and dryers for free (refrigerators cost extra; call 719-545-6472 for the current price). The Southside Landfill at 5715 W. Highway 78, about 3 miles west of Pueblo Blvd and Northern Ave, takes non-Freon appliances for $7 each, Monday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Loads must be tarped. Uncovered loads are charged double.

It's a refrigerator, freezer, or AC unit, and you were planning to take it to the dump. You can't. Southside turns away appliances with Freon, "not even gutted, tagged, or wires cut." That's not a reason to hire us specifically. It's a reason to make sure whoever takes it is sending it somewhere that recovers the refrigerant.

You just want the cheapest possible option and time doesn't matter. Some scrap metal operations and "free appliance pickup" services around Pueblo will take metal-heavy appliances at no charge because the steel is worth something to them. Service can be unreliable. Some of those websites are lead-generation pages that pass your number along. But if you're patient, free is free.

Where a Local Junk Hauler Actually Makes More Sense

The math flips once the job gets bigger or messier than "one appliance, already disconnected, one clear path to the door." Best Buy's $199.99 standalone service is capped at 2 large items and excludes a long list of things that are usually sitting right next to the old appliance:

  • Furniture: the old couch, the recliner, the dining set that went with the kitchen you're replacing
  • Fitness equipment: the treadmill in the basement is specifically excluded from the standalone service
  • Small kitchen appliances: blenders, coffee makers, toasters
  • Batteries, propane grills, musical instruments, books, DVDs, and CDs
  • Anything from a business: landlords clearing a rental between tenants, or property managers, can't use the standalone service at all

Picture a common Pueblo West scenario: a garage with a dead chest freezer, a second fridge, a washer that quit, a broken treadmill, and 10 years of boxes. Best Buy could take 2 of those items for $199.99, and you'd still have to deal with the rest. The same applies to bigger life events. Estate cleanouts, move-outs, and rental turnovers almost never involve just two appliances. Best Buy's service isn't built for that. A Quick Decision Guide for Pueblo West

Use this before you book anything:

1. Buying a replacement from Best Buy? Add the $49.99 haul-away. Done. 2. Old fridge or freezer still works, and you're a Black Hills Energy electric customer? Schedule the utility recycling pickup and take the rebate. 3. Non-Freon appliance, and you own a truck and a tarp? Appliance King (free for stoves, washers, dryers) or Southside Landfill ($7). 4. Exactly 2 large items, no furniture or fitness gear, all disconnected, and you're a homeowner rather than a business? Best Buy's $199.99 standalone service is a reasonable, predictable price. 5. More than 2 items, mixed junk, a tight timeline, or items that are hard to get out? Get a quote from a local hauler and compare it against $199.99 plus whatever you'd still have left over.

Getting the Most Out of Any Haul-Away Appointment

Whoever you hire, these steps prevent failed pickups and surprise charges:

  • Empty it completely. Food, shelves with dishes still on them, and clothes left in the dryer are grounds for refusal under Best Buy's terms, and they slow down any crew.
  • Defrost refrigerators and freezers 24 hours ahead and prop the doors open so they don't leak water through your house.
  • Measure the path out. Doorways, hallway turns, and the step down into the garage. A standard 36-inch-wide refrigerator doesn't care about your timeline.
  • Shut off water and gas first. Have gas lines capped by a qualified professional before appointment day.
  • Keep your receipts. If you're claiming a utility rebate on a new energy-efficient appliance, Black Hills requires a dated sales receipt rather than a bank statement.
