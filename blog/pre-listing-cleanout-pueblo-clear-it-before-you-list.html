PRE-LISTING CLEANOUT PUEBLO: CLEAR IT BEFORE YOU LIST

Your agent walks through, smiles, and says the house shows great — "just clear out the garage and tidy the back yard before we shoot photos." That's a bigger sentence than it sounds. Twelve years of accumulation doesn't fit in a minivan, and the Pueblo landfill is only open so many hours.

Here's how a pre-listing cleanout actually goes.

Start with what the camera sees

Photos sell the showing, and the showing sells the house. Buyers scrolling listings look at the garage, the side yard, and the back patio. A garage cleanout in Pueblo does more for a listing than new hardware on the cabinets, because a clear garage reads as square footage. Same for the yard — hauling yard debris in Pueblo, dead landscaping, old fence panels, and the pile of pavers you meant to use gives the lot back its shape.

The stuff that scares off buyers

Three things kill offers on Pueblo West properties: a dead hot tub, a shed that's half collapsed, and a fence line buried in brush. Buyers see cost and hassle. Hot tub removal in Pueblo is one of the toughest jobs there is — they're heavy, waterlogged, and full of fiberglass — and we've done dozens without tearing up a deck or a gate. Better it goes now than it comes up as a repair request after inspection.

Why the equipment matters

We run two 7-ton dump trailers and a skid steer with forks and a bucket. That's why a whole-house pre-listing clear-out is usually one visit. Skid steer work in Pueblo means concrete, rock, sheds, and heavy landscape debris get loaded in an hour instead of hand-carried all afternoon. Jobs other haulers turn down because of weight or access, we take.

Timing it right

Give yourself a week before photos, not a day. Book the cleanout, then do touch-up paint and cleaning after — no sense wiping down baseboards while we're still walking a couch out the door. Junk removal in Pueblo and Pueblo West is busiest in spring and late summer, so call ahead if your listing date is fixed. Same-day is often available if a slot opens.

What it costs
