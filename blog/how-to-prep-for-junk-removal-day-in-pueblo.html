HOW TO PREP FOR JUNK REMOVAL DAY IN PUEBLO

Most folks over-prepare for junk removal and stress about the wrong things. Here's what actually makes the day go faster — and what you can skip entirely.

Mark What Stays, Not What Goes

This is the one that matters. We can guess wrong on a bookshelf or a bike. Painter's tape, a sticky note, or a chalk X on anything you're keeping takes ten minutes and prevents the worst outcome of the day, which is us pulling something off the trailer.

If you'd rather just walk us through it when we arrive, that works too. Two minutes of "this pile goes, that corner stays" and we're clear.

Leave a Lane for the Trailer

We run two 7-ton dump trailers. That's a lot of truck to back down a driveway. If cars, a boat, or a basketball hoop can move before we get there, do it. On acreage in Pueblo West, tell us about soft spots, septic lids, or a gate we won't fit through — we'd rather know before we're on your grass.

For skid steer Pueblo jobs — concrete, heavy brush, anything that needs forks — we need roughly ten feet of gate clearance. Measure it and tell us on the call.

Don't Bother Hauling It Outside

You do not need to drag the washer up from the basement or wrestle the sectional through the front door. That's the labor you're hiring. Same with a garage cleanout Pueblo homeowners have been avoiding for three years, or a hot tub removal Pueblo job where the thing hasn't been touched since 2019. We come with the tools and the hands.

Separate the Stuff We Can't Take

A few things need a different destination: paint, solvents, propane tanks, fuel, and household chemicals. With 12+ years of hazardous-material handling behind us, we'll tell you straight what's a problem and where it should go. Set those aside and everything else moves quicker.

Yard Debris Piles Load Faster Loose

Bagged yard debris Pueblo customers set out is fine, but loose branch piles actually load quicker with the skid steer than bags do. Don't spend a Saturday bagging brush on our account.

Call Early If You Want It Gone Today

We're out every day 7 AM to 7 PM. Call before 11 and same-day is usually doable. After that we'll be honest about whether it's today or tomorrow.
