ESTATE CLEANOUT IN PUEBLO WEST: WHAT TO EXPECT

Clearing out a house after a parent passes, a relative moves into care, or a tenant leaves everything behind is a big job. It's also an emotional one. Here's how we handle estate cleanouts so you know what you're walking into.

We start with a free walk-through

Before anyone hauls anything, we come look at the property. We go room to room, check the garage, the basement, and the shed out back, and give you a flat price based on what's actually there. No hourly meter running, no guessing over the phone. You'll know the number before we lift a thing.

You decide what stays

Most estates have a mix — stuff the family wants, stuff that gets donated, and stuff that's just trash. You tell us the keep pile, and we work around it. If you're not sure yet, we can hold off on questionable items so nothing important leaves by accident. This is the part where being a local, family-owned crew matters. We don't rush you.

We bring the equipment for the heavy stuff

Old appliances, a waterbed, a workbench bolted to the wall, a hot tub on the patio — estate homes are full of things one or two people can't move. We run two 7-ton dump trailers and a skid steer with forks and a bucket, so the heavy and awkward loads other haulers turn down get cleared in the same visit. Skid steer Pueblo jobs like packed garages and overgrown yards are routine for us.

Inside and outside both

A full estate cleanout isn't just the house. We also clear garage cleanout Pueblo messes, haul off yard debris Pueblo storms left behind, and take whatever piled up around the property over the years. One crew, one price, one trip when we can.

Broom-clean when we leave

We finish by sweeping out so the place is ready to list, show, or hand back to a landlord. That's the standard whether it's a small rental or a packed family home.
