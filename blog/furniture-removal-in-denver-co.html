FURNITURE REMOVAL IN DENVER, CO?

If you searched "furniture removal services Denver CO," we should tell you up front that ClearPath Junk Removal does not serve Denver. We're based in Pueblo West, about 115 miles south on I-25, which is roughly a 1 hour 50 minute drive each way. If your couch is in Denver, a Denver hauler will be cheaper than us, and we'll explain why below. If your furniture is in Pueblo West or the Pueblo area, we can help. We usually haul furniture within 24–48 hours of your call. A single bulky piece typically costs $75–$200, and a whole-house load runs $450–$650. Most jobs take 30–90 minutes on site.

If you're moving from Pueblo West to Denver, read the section on that below. It's the most common reason people in our area search for Denver furniture removal, and it's where we can save you the most money.

Why a Denver Customer Shouldn't Hire Us

It's simple math. A truck trip from Pueblo West to Denver and back is about 230 miles, close to four hours of drive time. Fuel and a two-person crew's wages for those hours would add $200–$300 before we lifted anything. A local Denver company doesn't pay that, so you shouldn't either.

Denver also runs its own large-item collection for residents who get city trash service. If you qualify, that may cost you nothing except waiting for the scheduled date. Check with Denver Solid Waste Management before you pay any private company, including a Denver one.

The Pueblo-to-Denver Move: Get Rid of Furniture Before the Truck Comes

Every month we talk to Pueblo West households moving up to the Front Range. Many of them are about to pay to move furniture they plan to throw away once they arrive.

Movers charge for weight, time, or truck space. On an in-state move, crews commonly bill $120–$180 per hour for two movers, and they bill for the drive too. Some typical furniture weights:

  • A sleeper sofa weighs 200–350 lbs
  • A solid-wood dresser weighs 100–200 lbs
  • A recliner weighs 80–150 lbs
  • A queen mattress and box spring together weigh 90–130 lbs

If you're going to replace the sectional once you get to Denver, moving it 115 miles costs you twice. You pay to load it, haul it, and unload it. Then you pay a Denver company to take it away. Having it hauled from Pueblo West before moving day costs you once, and it can shrink your truck from a 26-foot to a 20-foot. That size change alone often saves $100–$200 on rental or truck fees.

> Get rid of furniture where you live now, not where you're going. Hauling it 115 miles just to throw it away is the most expensive way to get rid of it.

Our practical advice: schedule removal 3–7 days before the movers arrive. That leaves time to rearrange what's left without the empty rooms getting in the way of packing.

What Furniture Removal Costs in Pueblo West

These are the ranges you should expect from any reputable hauler in Southern Colorado, us included. The final price depends on how much space the items take in the truck, not just how many there are. We always give you a firm quote on site before we start.

  • Single small item (end table, office chair): usually covered by a $75–$100 minimum
  • Sofa, loveseat, or recliner: $100–$200 depending on size and whether it's a sleeper
  • Sectional (3+ pieces): $175–$300
  • Bedroom set (bed frame, dresser, two nightstands): $200–$350
  • Quarter truckload: about $175–$250
  • Full truckload (roughly 15 cubic yards, or a fully furnished 3-bedroom's worth of large furniture): $450–$650

What raises the price:

  • Stairs or a long carry from a back bedroom to the street
  • Taking pieces apart: built-in murphy beds, oversized sectionals, and gun safes are a separate category
  • Wet, moldy, or heavily soiled pieces that need extra handling

All of these ranges include loading, hauling, and disposal fees. If a quote doesn't include disposal, ask about it, because that's where surprise charges tend to show up.

When Furniture Removal ISN'T Worth Paying For

This section may lose us some jobs. We'd still rather you hear it from us than feel overcharged later.

1. You have one item and it's in good shape. A solid-wood dresser or a clean, stain-free couch can often be gone within 48 hours of a free listing on Facebook Marketplace or a local Pueblo West buy/sell group. If you can wait two days, paying us $100 makes no sense.

2. You have a truck and a friend. If the item fits in a pickup and two people can carry it, a trip to the transfer station or landfill usually costs far less than our minimum. Even if you rent a trailer for $30–$60 a day, you'll probably still come out ahead on a small load. You're paying us for labor, the truck, and time saved. If you have all three, you don't need us.

3. You expect everything to be donated. Many people assume most of their furniture will go to charity. It usually won't. Donation centers turn away upholstered furniture with stains, pet damage, tears, or smoke odor. They also turn away particleboard furniture that has been moved more than once, since it rarely survives a second trip. On a typical household load, maybe 20–40% of pieces can realistically be donated. We take donatable items to donation centers whenever we can, but we can't promise that your 15-year-old recliner will go to a new home.

4. The furniture has bed bugs. We'll be direct about this. Infested furniture has to be bagged or wrapped before anyone moves it, or it spreads through the house as it's carried out. Some haulers refuse these jobs outright. Others take them and don't mention the risk. Treat first, then call us, and tell us about it when you book.

5. You can wait for a free collection event. Some Pueblo County and municipal programs run periodic bulky-item or cleanup days. If your timeline is flexible and the item can sit in the garage for a month, check those first.

6. You're outside our area. We serve Pueblo West, Pueblo, and nearby Southern Colorado communities. Our [areas](/areas) page lists exactly where we go. If you're in Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, or Colorado Springs' northern suburbs, hire someone local to you.

When Hiring Us Is Clearly the Right Call

Paying a crew makes sense when any of these apply:

  • Weight and stairs. A 300-lb sleeper sofa on a split-level staircase is a common way to throw out a back or gouge drywall. Two trained people with straps and dollies handle it in about 10 minutes.
  • Volume. More than about 5–6 large pieces usually means 3+ trips in a pickup. That's most of a Saturday plus landfill fees for each trip.
  • A deadline. Closings, lease end dates, and moving-truck appointments don't move. We can usually come within 24–48 hours.
  • Estates and whole-house jobs. If you're clearing a parent's home, furniture is only part of the job. Our [estate cleanout Pueblo](/services/estate-cleanout-pueblo) service covers the whole house, including sorting what to keep, donate, and discard.
  • Mixed loads. If the furniture is going out along with a washer, garage clutter, or yard debris, one truck visit costs less than several separate ones.
