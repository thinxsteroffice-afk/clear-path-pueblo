GARAGE CLEANING COST: PUEBLO WEST

A one-car garage cleanout in Pueblo West runs $225–$450. A standard two-car garage runs $400–$850. A packed three-car or garage-plus-RV-bay job runs $850–$1,800. Those are full-service numbers — we sort, carry, load, sweep, and haul, and you don't touch anything. If you only need the hauling half (you pile it at the door, we take it), knock 25–35% off. Most Pueblo West garages land between $500 and $700 because the typical lot out here has a 2.5-car garage that's absorbed fifteen years of paint cans, dead swamp coolers, and half a shed's worth of overflow. Pricing is by volume in the truck, not by hour. A quarter-truck load is roughly $220; a full 16-cubic-yard truck runs about $780.

What Actually Sets Your Price

Volume is the meter. Everything else is a modifier. A junk removal truck holds about 16 cubic yards — roughly six full-size pickup beds, heaped. Here's the math we run in our heads when we walk your garage:

  • Floor space consumed. A 2-car garage is about 20' x 20' = 400 sq ft. Junk stacked 3 feet high across a quarter of that floor is roughly 100 cubic feet, or 3.7 cubic yards — a quarter truck.
  • Density penalty. Cardboard and plastic totes fill the truck fast and weigh nothing. Concrete pavers, old tile, and cast iron weigh out the truck at maybe 40% of its volume because we hit the axle limit first. Heavy material costs more per cubic yard, not less.
  • Sort labor. A garage where everything is already trash costs less than a garage where a family needs three keep/donate/toss piles made. Sorting adds 1–3 labor hours at roughly $65–$95/hour of crew time built into the quote.
  • Carry distance and stairs. A drive-up garage door is cheap. A basement-access garage or a 60-foot carry down a sloped Pueblo West driveway adds $50–$125.
  • Special-handling items. Freon appliances, tires, and paint each carry their own disposal cost — details below.

Two garages that look identical from the street can differ by $500 on those five variables alone. That's why nobody honest gives you a firm number over the phone without photos.

When You Should Not Hire Us

This is the part most junk removal sites leave out, so read it carefully. There are garages where paying us is a bad financial decision.

  • Under 2 cubic yards? Don't call us. Our minimum charge is around $150–$185, because a truck, fuel, two wages, and a landfill trip cost money whether we haul one couch or twelve. If your "junk" is a broken office chair, three bags, and an old TV, you're paying $150 for something a neighbor with a truck would do for a case of beer and $35 at the transfer station. Call us when the pile is real.
  • You have a truck, a free Saturday, and no back problems. A rented 6x12 dump trailer runs roughly $70–$110/day in Pueblo. Add $40–$60 per ton in disposal fees and a tank of gas. A two-car garage you're willing to spend two Saturdays on might cost you $250–$350 total, versus our $600. That's real money. The tradeoff is your weekend, your spine, and the fact that most people who plan two Saturdays finish in five.
  • You're doing this to "add value" before selling. A clean garage helps a listing photo. It does not move an appraisal. If you're spending $800 on a cleanout purely as a resale play, the return is soft and unmeasurable. Do it because you want your garage back.
  • Your stuff has actual resale value. A running snowblower, decent power tools, a functioning fridge — we haul those to a donation or recycling stream, and you get $0. A Saturday on Facebook Marketplace can net $300–$600 off a well-stocked garage. We're the right call for what's left after that, not instead of it.
  • You're mid-hoard and not ready. If pulling items out triggers a genuine crisis, a same-day crew is the wrong tool and the day will end badly for everyone. That's a slower, staged process — see [hoarding cleanout pueblo](/services/hoarding-cleanout-pueblo) — and it often needs family or a professional organizer in the room first.
  • Wait for a community cleanup if you can. Pueblo County and the Pueblo West Metro District periodically run cleanup events and drop-off days. Call the Metro District office before assuming one is scheduled, but if one lands in the next 30 days and your junk fits in your own vehicle, that's cheaper than we will ever be.

Fees That Catch People Off Guard

None of these are junk removal markup — they're pass-through disposal costs that any legitimate hauler pays and any lowball quote is hiding from you.

  • Freon appliances (fridges, freezers, window AC, dehumidifiers): $25–$45 each for certified refrigerant recovery. Required by federal law. If a quote doesn't mention it, ask where the unit is going. More detail on [appliance removal pueblo](/services/appliance-removal-pueblo).
  • Tires: roughly $7–$12 each for passenger tires, more for truck and tractor tires. A stack of eight in the corner is a $60–$90 line item.
  • Mattresses: $25–$40 each at most facilities.
  • Paint, solvents, motor oil, pesticides, propane tanks: we generally cannot take these, at any price. They need a household hazardous waste event or facility. This is the single most common surprise in a Pueblo West garage, because a 20-year garage almost always has a shelf of half-full paint cans. Latex paint you can dry out with kitty litter and dispose of as regular trash; oil-based and solvents you cannot.
  • Concrete, rock, sod, and dirt: these price by weight, separately, and a small pile can outweigh an entire truck of household junk. See [concrete rock removal pueblo](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo).

How to Cut Your Bill Before We Show Up

  • Pre-sort into three zones on the driveway: keep, donate, haul. This is the biggest lever you have — it routinely saves $100–$200.
  • Break down cardboard and disassemble furniture. A flat-packed shelving unit takes a fifth of the truck space of an assembled one. Volume is the meter; shrinking volume shrinks the bill.
  • Book off-peak. Late fall and mid-winter are slower than April–June, when every Pueblo West garage gets attacked at once. Weekday mornings beat Saturdays.
  • Combine jobs. One truck, one trip. Garage plus a shed, or garage plus [furniture removal pueblo](/services/furniture-removal-pueblo) from the house, costs less than two separate visits because you're only paying the mobilization once.
  • Schedule summer work before 10 a.m. At 4,700 feet with July highs near 95°F, an afternoon start means a slower crew and a longer job.

Text or email four photos: the garage from the open door, both back corners, and a close-up of anything heavy or hazardous. Any hauler who quotes a firm price from a phone description alone is either padding for the worst case or planning to renegotiate in your driveway. We give a written range before the truck rolls, and a firm price after a 5-minute walkthrough on site — before any work starts. If our on-site price is higher than the range you were quoted, you owe nothing and we leave.

A garage cleanout is typically 20–40% divertible if anyone bothers to sort it. Ask. The answer tells you a lot about who you're hiring.
