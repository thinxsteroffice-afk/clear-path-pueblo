GYM EQUIPMENT REMOVAL IN PUEBLO: HOW IT WORKS

Home gym felt like a great idea in January. By July it's a coat rack you can't walk around. If you're ready to get that space back, here's how junk removal Pueblo homeowners rely on handles the heavy stuff.

What we take

Just about anything you'd find in a home or garage gym:

  • Treadmills, ellipticals, and stair climbers
  • Weight benches, squat racks, and Smith machines
  • Loaded weight sets, dumbbells, and cast-iron plates
  • Cable and functional trainers (Bowflex, Total Gym, and the rest)
  • Stationary bikes and rowers

If it's exercise gear and you're done with it, we'll haul it.

Why this job isn't a one-person move

Gym equipment is deceptively heavy and built to be bolted together. A commercial-grade treadmill can run 300 pounds, and a full plate set adds up fast. Drag it up a flight of basement stairs the wrong way and you'll scuff walls, tear carpet, or hurt your back. We've done this plenty — 12-plus years of hauling heavy, awkward loads around Pueblo. We bring enough hands, break machines down when a doorway or stairwell is tight, and pad the tight corners on the way out.

How the job goes

1. You call or text. Tell us what you've got and where it sits — basement, garage, spare room. 2. We quote it straight. Price is based on how much space it takes in the trailer and the labor to get it out. No vague estimates, no surprise fees at the end. 3. We haul it. Point at the gear. We carry it out, load our dump trailer, and sweep up after ourselves. 4. It goes to the right place. Usable equipment gets donated or resold when we can; the rest is disposed of responsibly.

Same-day when you need it

We're open every day, 7 AM to 7 PM, and same-day pickup is often on the table if you catch us early. Whether it's one treadmill or a whole garage gym cleanout, we'll get it done in one trip.
