SHED DEMO & REMOVAL COST BY SIZE

Shed demo and removal in Pueblo West runs $375 to $650 for a typical 8x10 wood shed, $600 to $900 for a 10x12, and $1,100 to $1,800 for a 12x20 barn-style building with a loft. Metal utility sheds start around $250. That price covers tear-down, loading, hauling, disposal fees, and raking the footprint clean. Most jobs take a two-person crew 90 minutes to 3 hours and produce 1.5 to 3 tons of debris. ClearPath Junk Removal serves Pueblo West, Pueblo, Colorado City, Rye, Avondale, and Beulah, and we can usually be on site within 24 to 72 hours. Concrete slab removal is a separate line item, typically $350 to $900 depending on thickness and access. Details, exclusions, and the cases where you should skip us entirely are below.

What "demo and removal" actually includes

The phrase gets used loosely. Some outfits mean they will drop the walls and leave the pile. Here is the full scope we quote as a single price on a [shed demolition and removal](/services/shed-demolition-removal-pueblo) job:

  • Disconnect and safe-off. Any run of buried electrical to the shed gets cut back and capped. If it is live conduit off your panel, we stop and tell you to call an electrician. We do not pull permits or do line work.
  • Contents out first. A shed is a junk removal job wearing a wooden hat. The mower, the paint cans, the 40 pounds of rusted hand tools all have to come out before the walls do. Contents hauling is included up to roughly one pickup bed; past that we price it as a [general junk removal](/services/junk-removal-pueblo) add-on.
  • Structural teardown. Roof, then walls, then floor deck, then joists and skids. Reverse of how it went up.
  • Fastener and anchor sweep. Ground anchors, rebar pins, and the several hundred nails and screws that end up in the dirt. This is the step that separates a real crew from a guy with a sledgehammer.
  • Debris out and site raked. Everything leaves in one trip on a trailer. You get a flat rectangle, not a landscaping project.

What is not automatically included: the slab, the gravel base under the slab, hazardous material abatement, and fence panels you want removed at the same time. Each of those is priced separately and each is explained below.

The Pueblo West specifics that change the price

Pueblo West is not Pueblo. The lot sizes, the soil, and the rules are different, and all three show up on the invoice.

Lot size and access help you. Most Pueblo West parcels run 0.5 to 2.5 acres, and the majority let us back a truck and a 16-foot trailer within 20 feet of the shed. That is the cheapest possible scenario. When a shed sits behind a 48-inch gate and every scrap has to be hand-carried 80 feet to the street, add $100 to $200 for labor.

Wind is a real scheduling constraint. Gusts over 45 mph are common here in spring, and a shed with the roof off and three walls standing is a sail. We will not leave a partially demolished structure overnight in a wind advisory. That means we either finish the job in one visit or we do not start it that day.

Soil rutting. The caliche and clay out here sets up like pavement dry and turns to grease within an hour of rain. If we need a machine for a heavy slab or a large barn, wet ground means we reschedule rather than carve ruts across your yard. Our [skid steer services](/services/skid-steer-services-pueblo) get used on maybe one shed job in five, almost always slab-related.

Permits and covenants. Pueblo West is unincorporated Pueblo County, so building permits go through Pueblo County Planning and Development, not a city office. Small accessory structures generally fall under a square-footage exemption for demolition, but that threshold has been revised before, so confirm the current number before you schedule anything over roughly 200 square feet. Separately, the Pueblo West Metropolitan District enforces covenants on the district's residential lots. Removing a shed rarely triggers anything, but replacing it does. Ask about the replacement before you demolish the old one, not after.

> The shed is the easy part. The slab, the anchors, and the two decades of stuff inside it are where the real time and money go.

The slab question

Roughly 60 percent of the sheds we remove sit on a poured pad. You have three options.

Leave it. Free. Fine if you are putting a new shed, a hot tub, or a grill station in the same spot.

Break and haul it. A 10x12 pad poured 4 inches thick is about 1.5 cubic yards, or close to 3 tons of concrete. Priced with the shed, that is usually $350 to $600. If it is 6 inches thick with wire mesh or rebar, expect $700 to $900 and a machine. See [concrete and rock removal](/services/concrete-rock-removal-pueblo) for standalone pad jobs.

Break it and bury it. Do not. Buried concrete becomes the next owner's inspection problem and it will not settle evenly.

One thing almost nobody mentions: under the slab is usually 3 to 6 inches of compacted road base. Pull the concrete and you have a gravel rectangle that grass will not grow through. Budget another $150 to $300 for base removal and topsoil if you want lawn there, or plan on a gravel-friendly use for the space.

When you should NOT hire us

This section costs us jobs. It is here anyway.

  • Your shed is small metal and you own a socket set. An 8x8 Arrow or Sears metal shed is bolted, not built. Two people with a nut driver can have it flat in 90 minutes and stacked in a truck bed. Paying $250 to $400 for that is paying us for the dump run and your Saturday. If your time is worth less than about $40 an hour on a weekend, do it yourself.
  • The shed is intact and sellable. A structurally sound 10x12 with a good roof moves on local marketplace listings for $200 to $500, "you haul it." That is a $900 swing versus paying for demolition. Listing it costs you a week of messages and a few no-shows, but the math is not close. We will tell you on the estimate if we think yours is worth listing.
  • You already have a dumpster on site. If you are mid-remodel with a 20-yard container in the driveway, your marginal cost to demo the shed yourself is near zero. Hiring a hauler on top of a rented dumpster is paying twice for disposal.
  • You need the shed moved, not destroyed. We demolish. Relocating a shed intact requires a mule or shed-moving trailer and a company that specializes in it. Hire them directly.
  • There is confirmed asbestos. Sheds built before roughly 1989 can have transite siding or asbestos roofing panels. Colorado regulations require a licensed abatement contractor once the affected material passes about 32 square feet. We are not an abatement contractor. If a test comes back positive, we stop, you pay nothing, and you call an abatement firm first. Paying us to show up before you have that answer wastes a trip.
  • Budget under $200. We have a minimum. Below it, the drive, the dump fee, and two people's time do not clear cost. Combine the shed with a [garage cleanout](/services/garage-cleanout-pueblo) or other hauling and the number starts making sense.

The failure modes people find out about too late

Mice relocate into your house. A shed that has stood for 15 years in Pueblo West is a rodent apartment building. Tear it down and those animals move 30 to 60 feet, which is often your garage. Set traps the same week. Also, dry rodent nests are the hantavirus risk in southern Colorado. Our crews wear respirators for exactly this reason and you should too if you are doing it yourself.

Wasps and black widows. Roughly one shed in three has an active nest in the eaves or under the floor deck between May and September. We will treat and wait rather than open a nest over your kids' play area.

Buried surprises. Old sprinkler laterals, a forgotten septic cleanout, and dog-buried debris all live under sheds. We hand-dig anything ambiguous rather than machine through it, which is slower and occasionally means a change order.

The dead rectangle. Where the shed sat, nothing grows for a season or two. Compacted soil, no light, no water. Plan for soil amendment or hardscape.

DIY time overrun. The honest DIY estimate for a 10x12 wood shed with a shingle roof is 12 to 16 person-hours plus two or three trips to the landfill with a truck and trailer. The roofing alone on that shed weighs around 350 pounds. Most people budget a morning and lose a weekend.
