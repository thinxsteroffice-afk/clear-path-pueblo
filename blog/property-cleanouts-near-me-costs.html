PROPERTY CLEANOUTS NEAR ME: COSTS

Property cleanouts near me in Pueblo West run $275 to $850 for a single-room or garage job, $900 to $2,400 for a full house, and $2,500 to $6,000+ for a hoarding-level or multi-outbuilding property on acreage. ClearPath Junk Removal serves Pueblo West, Pueblo, Colorado City, Rye, Avondale, Boone, and Beulah. A property cleanout means we empty a house, rental, garage, storage unit, or land parcel of everything the owner is leaving behind, sweep it out, and haul it to the Pueblo County landfill, the transfer station, or a donation partner. Most Pueblo West cleanouts are booked within 48 hours and finished in a single day. You get a fixed price before we touch anything.

What a Pueblo West Property Cleanout Actually Includes

A cleanout is not the same as junk pickup. Junk pickup is "take this couch." A cleanout is "the tenant left in July and I need the whole thing empty by Friday."

Our standard property cleanout covers:

  • Everything inside — furniture, mattresses, clothing, boxes, food waste, pet damage, garage shelving, the stuff under the stairs nobody wanted to open.
  • Everything outside — the pile behind the shed, the dead swamp cooler, tires, scrap metal, [yard debris](/services/yard-debris-removal-pueblo), and the trampoline with a torn mat.
  • Appliance and fixture removal — refrigerators, ranges, water heaters, washer/dryer sets. Sealed-system appliances with refrigerant carry a $35 to $50 handling fee each because Pueblo County requires certified evacuation before scrapping.
  • Broom-swept finish — floors swept, nail-free, ready for a photographer or a flooring crew. We do not deep-clean, sanitize, or paint.
  • Disposal receipts — itemized dump tickets on request, which matters if you're billing an estate, an insurer, or a tenant's deposit.

Pueblo West's lot sizes change the math. A one-acre parcel off McCulloch or Purcell frequently has two or three outbuildings, a horse shelter, and a decade of accumulated fence panels. That is not the same job as a 1,400 sq ft ranch in Pueblo's Belmont neighborhood, and it should not be the same price.

Real Pueblo West Pricing, Not a "Free Quote" Runaround

Volume drives price. We price by how much of a 15-cubic-yard trailer you fill, plus labor time and disposal weight.

| Job | Typical range | Time on site | |---|---|---| | Single room or small [garage cleanout](/services/garage-cleanout-pueblo) | $275 – $550 | 1.5 – 3 hrs | | Full garage, packed | $550 – $850 | 3 – 5 hrs | | 10x20 storage unit | $400 – $750 | 2 – 4 hrs | | 3-bed house, tenant left furniture | $1,100 – $2,400 | 6 – 10 hrs | | Hoarding-level, biohazard-free | $2,800 – $6,500 | 2 – 4 days | | Acreage with 2+ outbuildings | $3,000 – $9,000 | 2 – 5 days |

Add-ons that genuinely change the number: refrigerant appliances at $35 to $50 each, tires at $8 to $14 apiece because Pueblo County charges by unit, and concrete or dirt at roughly $45 to $75 per ton since that goes to a different facility than household waste. A [hot tub](/services/hot-tub-removal-pueblo) adds $350 to $650 depending on whether it has to come over a fence.

The Honest Part: When You Should Not Hire Us

This section costs us jobs. It's still the right advice.

Skip the cleanout crew and rent a dumpster if you have time and a body. A 20-yard roll-off in Pueblo West runs about $425 to $600 for a 7-day rental, including a tonnage allowance around 3 to 4 tons. If your job is one trailer's worth of non-heavy household goods, and you have two weekends and someone under 50 with a working back, you will save $500 to $1,200 doing it yourself. That is a real number and we are not going to pretend otherwise. The dumpster route falls apart when the driveway can't take a roll-off, when the HOA-equivalent covenants in some Pueblo West filings restrict container placement, or when you're 900 miles away in another state.

Do not hire us for a small load. Our minimum is $150 to $175 because the truck, the fuel to get out to Pueblo West, the landfill gate fee, and two people's time don't scale below that. If you have one mattress and a recliner, you're better served by our single-item [furniture removal](/services/furniture-removal-pueblo) or [mattress removal](/services/mattress-removal-pueblo), or by hauling it yourself to the Pueblo transfer station for around $30 to $45.

We are not the right call for these, and we will tell you on the phone:

  • Biohazard, meth contamination, or unattended death. Colorado requires a licensed remediation contractor for meth-affected properties under state regulation, and the testing and cleanup runs $5,000 to $30,000. We are not that contractor. Calling us first wastes a day.
  • Anything with asbestos. Pueblo housing stock from before 1980 commonly has asbestos in floor tile mastic, popcorn ceilings, and duct wrap. Colorado requires abatement by a certified contractor above regulated thresholds. We will stop work and tell you to test. That's a $300 to $700 test and a schedule hit of a week or more.
  • Live animals, active infestation, or standing sewage. Get pest control or a plumber in first. A rodent infestation that needs treatment before entry adds $250 to $900 and three to five days.
  • Jobs where you expect us to appraise and resell your stuff. We are not estate liquidators. If there's a 1968 Mustang under a tarp or a genuine coin collection, get an appraiser before we arrive. We have hauled things worth more than the invoice and it makes nobody happy.

The failure modes you should know about. The most common one is scope creep: a customer books a "garage" and on arrival the garage is empty because everything got moved to the back yard, the shed, and a fifth-wheel. The price changes. The second is the locked-storage surprise, where a [storage unit cleanout](/services/storage-unit-cleanout-pueblo) turns out to be 60% water-damaged particleboard that weighs triple what it looks like, pushing disposal cost up $150 to $400. The third is weather. Pueblo West gets 40 to 55 mph gusts in spring, and we will not load an open trailer in that. Expect a reschedule, not a discount.

One more thing that will annoy you. We do not take paint, motor oil, pesticides, pool chemicals, propane tanks, or car batteries. Pueblo County runs household hazardous waste drop-off events, and quantities are limited per household. Those items get set aside in your garage and stay there. Budget an afternoon for it.

Landlords, Realtors, and Foreclosure Timelines

Turnaround matters more than price for this group. A vacant rental in Pueblo West at $1,500 a month costs you about $50 a day. Waiting nine days for a cheaper hauler to save $300 loses money.

For evictions, Colorado's writ of restitution process means the sheriff gives a short window to remove property, and in many Colorado jurisdictions tenant belongings placed at the curb become the landlord's problem within roughly 24 to 48 hours. We schedule [foreclosure and eviction cleanouts](/services/foreclosure-eviction-cleanout-pueblo) same-day or next-day when you have a writ date, and we photograph everything before it goes in the trailer. That photo set has settled more than one deposit dispute.

Realtors: a cleaned-out, broom-swept vacant listing photographs better and typically shortens time on market. We can hit a Thursday cleanout for a Saturday open house if you book by Tuesday.

Estates, Downsizing, and the Emotional Math

An [estate cleanout](/services/estate-cleanout-pueblo) is the hardest version of this job, and the mistake families make is booking too early. Give siblings a hard deadline to claim items, then book us for the day after. We have shown up to houses where three family members were still negotiating over a dining set at 8 a.m. and the crew stood around for two hours you paid for.

What we do that helps: we run a donation pass. Serviceable furniture, working appliances, and clothing go to Pueblo-area donation partners rather than the landfill. Typical diversion on a residential cleanout is 20% to 40% by volume. Ask for the donation receipt if you're itemizing deductions.

For [hoarding situations](/services/hoarding-cleanout-pueblo), plan on three days minimum for a 1,200 sq ft home, and plan for the homeowner to be present but not in the room. Sorting cash and documents out of the debris is real work. We've found $2,300 in an envelope inside a cereal box. It goes back to the family, every time.

How to Get an Accurate Number in 10 Minutes

  • Send 8 to 12 photos, including the worst corner and every outbuilding. Wide shots, not close-ups.
  • Measure or estimate square footage and count the appliances.
  • Tell us the access truth. Gravel drive, soft ground, a gate 8 feet wide, a septic field we shouldn't drive over. Pueblo West has all of these, and a truck stuck in a leach field is a bad day for both of us.
  • Name your deadline. Same-week costs more than three weeks out, usually 10% to 20%.
  • Ask what's excluded. Every honest quote has exclusions. If ours doesn't list any, we made a mistake.
