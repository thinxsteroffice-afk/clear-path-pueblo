Repair or Replace? The Numbers to Run First

Before paying for a service call or a haul-away, work through this. Repair techs and consumer groups commonly use two rules of thumb:

  • The 50% rule. If the repair costs more than half the price of a comparable new appliance, replace it.
  • The halfway-through-its-life rule. If the appliance is past about half its expected lifespan, repairs usually aren't worth it. Typical lifespans:
  • Refrigerators: 10 to 15 years
  • Washers and dryers: 10 to 13 years
  • Dishwashers: about 9 to 10 years
  • Gas ranges: often 15 years or more, because there's less to break

Take a refrigerator that's 4 years old and needs a $250 fan motor. That's usually worth fixing. Take a 12-year-old top-freezer model with a failed sealed system, where the quote is often close to what a new fridge costs. That one should go.

> A repair that buys you two more years on a 13-year-old refrigerator usually costs more per month of use than a new unit does.

Pueblo West adds a local problem: hard water. Minerals build up in dishwasher pumps, washer inlet valves and water heater tanks, so those appliances often fail earlier here than the national averages above suggest. If your dishwasher is 7 years old and already on its second pump, that pattern will likely continue.

When You Should NOT Hire Us to Haul Your Appliance

We'd rather lose the job than have you pay for something you don't need. Here's when paying a junk removal crew is a bad deal:

1. You have a truck and the appliance has no Freon. Stoves, washers, dryers, dishwashers and water heaters don't contain refrigerant. The Southside Landfill at 5715 W State Highway 78 takes them for $7.00 apiece. It's open Monday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. If you have a pickup, a dolly and a friend, $7 plus gas beats any haul-away fee. Tarp or strap the load: the landfill charges double for uncovered or unsecured loads, no exceptions.

2. You own a refrigerator, freezer or window AC and can wait. Black Hills Energy, Pueblo's electric company, runs an appliance recycling program for its Colorado electric customers: - It pays $50 for an old refrigerator, stand-alone freezer or window air conditioner. - You get an extra $50 for turning in a working window AC at the same pickup as a fridge or freezer. - Refrigerators must be between 10 and 30 cubic feet. - Each household gets 2 pickups per calendar year, covering up to 7 appliances total (2 refrigerators, 2 freezers and 3 room ACs). - Pickups are generally scheduled about once a month.

If the fridge can sit in your garage for a few weeks, the utility pays you instead of you paying someone. Before you count on the money, check the current rules at 1-888-277-0525.

3. You're buying a new appliance anyway. Many stores will haul away the old unit when they deliver the new one, for a small fee or free. Ask before you check out. It's often cheaper than booking a separate pickup.

4. It can still be fixed cheaply. If the numbers above say repair, call one of the local repair shops. Hauling away a 3-year-old washer that needs a $40 lid switch wastes the appliance and your money.

5. It's hooked to a gas line. A gas range or gas dryer needs a licensed plumber or gas technician to shut off and cap the line before anyone moves it. A junk removal crew is not a substitute for that. Get the line capped first, or you'll pay for a return trip.

6. You have a single item and a scrap-minded neighbor. Pueblo County has active scrap metal buyers. A steel washer or dryer set out with a "free" sign on a Pueblo West lot often disappears within a day or two.

When Hauling It Away Actually Makes Sense

Paying for removal is the right call when the free and cheap options don't fit:

  • The appliance has Freon and you can't wait a month. Southside Landfill refuses refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners, even if they're gutted, tagged or have the wires cut. The refrigerant has to be recovered at a facility that's set up to do it, which you can't arrange with a pickup truck and a free afternoon.
  • It doesn't qualify for the Black Hills program. That includes refrigerators under 10 or over 30 cubic feet, units that use ammonia or SO2 refrigerant, or a household that already used its 2 pickups this year.
  • It's in a basement, on a second floor or behind tight doorways. A side-by-side refrigerator can weigh 300 pounds or more. Hauling one up basement stairs is a common way to get a hernia or gouge your drywall.
  • It's one piece of a bigger job. If you're also clearing out a garage, a rental turnover or a parent's house, removing the appliance with everything else costs less than scheduling each piece separately. See our [estate cleanout pueblo](/services/estate-cleanout-pueblo) and [garage cleanout pueblo](/services/garage-cleanout-pueblo) pages.
  • The deadline is set by someone else. Closing dates, move-out inspections and landlord walkthroughs don't wait for a monthly utility pickup.

For single appliances, our [appliance removal pueblo](/services/appliance-removal-pueblo) page covers what we take and how pickup works. For mixed loads, see [junk removal pueblo](/services/junk-removal-pueblo).

A Quick Decision Checklist for Pueblo West Homeowners

Work through these in order and stop at the first "yes":

1. Is it under about half its expected lifespan, with a repair quote under 50% of replacement cost? Call a local repair company. 2. Is it a working-age fridge, freezer or window AC that fits the Black Hills rules, and can you wait a few weeks? Schedule the utility pickup and collect the $50. 3. Are you buying a replacement? Ask the store to take the old one. 4. Is it a non-Freon appliance, and do you have a truck? Take it to Southside Landfill for $7. 5. None of the above? Get a haul-away quote, and make sure the quote says where Freon units end up.

What to Ask Any Company Before Booking

Whether you call a repair shop or a hauler, ask these before you agree to anything:

  • Is the service call or trip fee credited toward the repair or job?
  • Where do refrigerant appliances go after pickup? A company that can't answer this is a red flag, since the landfill won't take them.
  • Is the price all-in, or will stairs, disconnection or wait time add to it?
  • Do you actually come to Pueblo West? Some Pueblo and Colorado Springs companies charge extra for addresses out on the mesa.
