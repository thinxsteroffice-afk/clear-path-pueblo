About Junk Removal at ClearPath

Most Pueblo junk calls start the same way: something big and heavy has been sitting for months because it won't fit in a truck bed and it takes two strong people to move. That's the whole job for us. We come to the house, look at what needs to go, give you a price before we touch anything, and then load it — out of the basement, down the stairs, through the side gate, off the back patio. You don't lift a thing.

We work all over the city and the surrounding county: the older brick homes and tight alleys around the Mesa Junction and Bessemer, the Northside and Belmont neighborhoods near CSU Pueblo, University Park, Highland Park, Eastwood Heights, Pueblo Boulevard rentals turning over between tenants, and the big lots out in Pueblo West where junk tends to collect behind the shop or along the back fence line. Alley-access properties and narrow driveways are routine for us — we bring the truck and trailer that fits.

What to think about on cost: junk removal is priced by how much space the load takes in the truck, plus extra for unusually heavy material like concrete, dirt, or tile. A single couch or a fridge is a small job. A packed two-car garage is a full truck. Refrigerators, freezers, and AC units carry a disposal fee at the transfer station because of the refrigerant, and mattresses have their own fee — we'll tell you that upfront rather than surprising you at the end. Send a photo by text and you'll usually have a number back the same hour.

How fast we get there: we're based in Pueblo West off Highway 50, so most of Pueblo is a 15–25 minute drive — the North Side and Pueblo West are quickest, downtown, Bessemer and the East Side run a little longer. Same-day service is available most days across Pueblo County, and we run 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM seven days a week, with 24-hour availability for urgent jobs like a closing, an inspection, or a rental that has to be empty tomorrow.
