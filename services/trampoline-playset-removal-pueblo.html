About Trampoline & Playset Removal at ClearPath

Backyard trampolines and playsets are built to survive weather and kids, which is exactly what makes them a pain to remove. The bolts rust, the springs are under tension, and around here they're usually staked or concreted into ground that's hard as rock by August. Wrestling one apart with a socket set on a Saturday rarely goes well. ClearPath brings the crew and the tools to take it down safely and haul it off in one visit.

We remove full-size and kids' trampolines including the frame, mat, springs, and net enclosure, plus every style of playset — metal swing sets, wood cedar forts, slides, climbing walls, and monkey bars. When a unit is anchored with ground stakes or set in concrete footings, we dig the anchors out so you're not left with tripping hazards or stubs in the lawn. Everything loads into our dump trailer and leaves with us.

Most trampoline and playset removals in Pueblo and Pueblo West are quick, same-day jobs, and we'll quote the flat price from a photo before we arrive. If you're staging a home to sell or clearing space for something new, we'll make sure the footprint is clean and level. Call or text (719) 334-4581 to schedule.
