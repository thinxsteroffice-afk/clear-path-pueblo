About Foreclosure & Eviction Cleanouts at ClearPath

A tenant or former owner who leaves in a hurry rarely leaves a property clean. Landlords, property managers, realtors, and lenders in Pueblo call ClearPath for exactly this: the post-eviction and post-foreclosure trash-out that stands between an empty unit and a rentable, sellable one. We understand that vacancy is expensive, so we prioritize these jobs and turn them around fast — frequently within the same week you call.

We remove everything left behind: furniture, mattresses, clothing, boxes, appliances, and the general trash and debris that accumulate when a place is abandoned. Our crew sorts as we go, setting aside anything genuinely usable for donation and disposing of the rest responsibly. When we're done the property is broom-swept and empty, ready for your cleaners, painters, and flooring crews — which shortens the whole make-ready timeline and gets the unit earning again.

We work well with the people who manage these situations: reliable scheduling around your availability, lockbox and access coordination, clear itemized invoices for your accounting, and the discretion these transitions call for. All work is fully insured. Whether it's a single-unit eviction or a full foreclosure cleanout, call (719) 334-4581 and we'll get the property rent-ready.
