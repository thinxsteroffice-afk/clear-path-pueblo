About Shed Demolition at ClearPath

Old sheds don't come down neatly. Years of Pueblo sun and wind leave the wood brittle, the roof sagging, and the whole thing full of rusty nails and screws. Trying to knock one down yourself usually means a weekend of splinters, a trailer you don't own, and a landfill run you'd rather skip. ClearPath handles the entire job — demolition, loading, hauling, and cleanup — so you get your yard back without lifting a board.

We take down every kind of shed: wood-frame garden sheds, rusted metal storage buildings, plastic and resin kits, chicken coops, playhouses, and small pole barns. If the structure sits on a concrete slab, we can leave the pad clean for reuse or break it out and remove the concrete too. For bigger outbuildings we bring the skid steer to make the demolition faster and safer. Everything gets separated for the appropriate disposal or recycling.

Because these jobs vary so much, pricing depends on size, materials, and whether the slab comes out — but we always quote the full number upfront from photos and dimensions, and demolition work is fully insured. When we're done, we sweep the site with a magnet to pull stray nails so you can walk it barefoot. Call (719) 334-4581 to get your old shed gone.
