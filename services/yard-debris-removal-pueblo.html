About Yard Debris Removal at ClearPath

Pueblo's wind storms and hot summers do a number on landscaping. When you've got a pile of branches after a windstorm, a yard full of brush you've been meaning to clear, or the leftovers from a finished landscape project — ClearPath hauls it all. We can come out the same day, fit our 7-ton dump trailer into most driveways, and take a full load in a single trip.

For bigger jobs — full acre-lot brush clearing, fence-line tear-down, or major landscape overhauls — we bring the skid steer with forks and bucket. We can clear, grade, and haul in one visit, which is dramatically cheaper than three different contractors.

We dispose of everything responsibly. Green waste goes to compost facilities, recyclables are sorted, and the rest goes to proper disposal. No illegal dumping, no shortcuts.
