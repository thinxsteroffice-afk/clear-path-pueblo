About Hoarding Cleanouts at ClearPath

A hoarding situation is never just about stuff — it's about a person and a family, and it deserves to be handled with care. ClearPath approaches every hoarding cleanout in Pueblo the same way: quietly, respectfully, and without a shred of judgment. Whether we're helping an adult child clear a parent's home, working alongside a case manager, or supporting someone ready to reclaim their own space, our job is to make a hard day easier.

We work methodically, room by room. Before anything leaves, we look for what matters — identification and financial documents, family photos, jewelry, cash tucked in books, and irreplaceable keepsakes — and set it aside for you. Then we clear the clutter, furniture, spoiled goods, and debris, using gloves, masks, and safe procedures for the heavy dust, mold, pests, or biohazards these homes often involve. We can move at a gentle, phased pace or clear the whole property in one focused effort, whichever the situation calls for.

Discretion is built into how we operate: our trailers are unmarked and our crew is low-profile, so the process stays private. Because these projects vary enormously in scope, we quote after a walk-through or a set of photos, and the work is fully insured. If you or someone you love is facing a hoarding cleanout in Pueblo or Pueblo West, call (719) 334-4581 for a confidential, no-pressure conversation.
