About Estate Cleanout at ClearPath

An estate cleanout is never just a junk job — it's usually happening at a difficult time, often with family spread across the country and a realtor or probate deadline looming. ClearPath has done many of these across Pueblo and Pueblo West, and we approach every one the same way: patiently, respectfully, and without rushing you.

We clear the entire property — every room, closet, the garage, sheds, and the yard — and leave it broom-clean and ready to list or transfer. As we work, we set aside anything that looks personal or valuable (photos, documents, jewelry, keepsakes) for the family to look through before anything leaves. Usable furniture and household goods are sorted for donation to local Pueblo charities, and we can provide a donation receipt list on request.

You don't have to be on-site the whole time, and you don't have to have everything sorted before we arrive — that's what we're there for. We'll walk the property with you (or over video if you're out of town), give you a flat quote for the full job, and schedule it around your deadline. Estate cleanout pricing depends on the size of the home and the volume; we'll always quote the whole job upfront, with no surprises.
