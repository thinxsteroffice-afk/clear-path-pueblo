About Above-Ground Pool Removal at ClearPath

Above-ground pools are great until they're not — the liner splits, the frame rusts, the water turns green, and suddenly you've got an eyesore that's also a safety concern with kids and pets around. Taking one down is a bigger job than most people expect: there's water to drain the right way, sharp metal panels, a heavy liner, and usually a deck or fence built around it. ClearPath does the whole thing start to finish.

Our crew drains the pool responsibly, disassembles the wall panels and top rails, rolls and cuts the liner, and pulls the pump, filter, ladder, and any leftover chemicals. If you built a wood deck, laid pavers, or fenced the pool in, we remove that too and haul it all in our dump trailers. When we're finished, the pad or footprint is cleared and ready for sod, gravel, xeriscape, or whatever you want to put there next.

Every pool is a little different, so we quote from photos and the pool's size and setup — and the teardown is fully insured. Late spring through early fall is our busy season for these, so if you want it gone before summer, book ahead. Call or text (719) 334-4581 and we'll get your old pool cleared out.
