About Mattress Removal at ClearPath

Getting rid of a mattress in Pueblo is harder than it should be. Curbside trash pickup won't take them, most transfer stations charge a special bulky-item fee, and stuffing a queen into a car rarely works. ClearPath makes it simple: we come to you, pull the mattress and box spring out of whatever room they're in, and haul them away — often the same day you call.

We remove every kind of sleep surface — pillow-top and memory-foam mattresses, box springs, bunk beds, crib mattresses, adjustable bases, and the frames and headboards that go with them. If you're replacing a bedroom set, we'll take the dresser and nightstands in the same load so you're not left with a pile in the garage. Because we bring two people and a dump trailer, stairs and narrow hallways are never a problem.

Mattresses are bulky but light, so pricing is straightforward — most single mattress-and-box-spring removals in Pueblo and Pueblo West run in the low double digits, and we'll give you the exact number from a photo before we show up. Where a mattress recycler will accept the materials, we route the steel and foam there instead of the landfill. Call or text (719) 334-4581 and we'll get your old bed gone.
