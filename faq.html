FAQ · Pueblo West, CO

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Pricing, scheduling, what we take, and how it works — straight answers from the ClearPath family.

Do you offer same-day junk removal in Pueblo?

Yes — same-day service is available when capacity allows, especially if you call before 11 AM. Text us a photo for the fastest quote turnaround.

How much does junk removal cost?

It depends on volume and the type of items. Most single-item removals (couch, mattress, appliance) are $75–$150. Full garage cleanouts typically run $300–$800. Hot tub removal is $250–$600 depending on size and access. We always quote upfront — no surprise charges.

Will you take a hot tub?

Absolutely — hot tub removal is one of the most common things we get called for. We disassemble, haul, and dispose of everything. Fully insured to protect your deck and patio.

Do you bring the equipment, or do I need to help?

We bring everything. Two 7-ton dump trailers, skid steer with forks and bucket, and the crew. You point. We lift, load, and haul. You don't lift a thing.

Where are you located?

291 W Northampton Dr, Pueblo West, CO 81007. We're based in Pueblo West but serve Pueblo, Avondale, Cañon City, and all of Pueblo County.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit card, debit card, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is on completion of the job.

Do you do hoarder or sensitive cleanouts?

Yes — with experience, discretion, and respect. We've handled some of the toughest cleanouts in Pueblo County. We work at your pace and treat every situation with sensitivity.

Are you insured?

Fully insured for liability and property damage. We also have 12+ years of hazardous material handling experience for any items requiring special disposal.

Will you donate items if I want them to go to charity?

Yes — at no extra charge. We can sort donatable items and drop them at Pueblo charities (Salvation Army, Habitat ReStore, etc.) as part of the haul.

Do you do dirt work and excavation too?

Yes — we run a skid steer with forks and bucket for excavation, grading, brush clearing, fence demo, and xeriscape prep. Often the same crew can haul + dirt work on the same visit.

Do you serve commercial customers?

Yes — property managers, contractors, real estate agents, and small businesses. We do construction debris, eviction cleanouts, foreclosure cleanouts, and recurring commercial waste removal.

How fast can you get to me?

Same-day if you call before 11 AM and we have capacity. Otherwise next-day is almost always available. Text photos for the fastest quote — sometimes we can route a truck the same hour.

Still have a question?

Call or text us — we pick up.
